After a visit, your clinic can send the patient a link to rate how they were looked after, from 1 to 5. If they're happy, they're invited to leave a Google review; if not, they're asked what you could do better, and their answer goes only to the clinic. The survey is part of **Growth** and is switched on as an automation: see [Reputation and Google reviews](/articles/growth/reputation).

## What the patient sees

::help-video{clip="portal--survey" caption="The patient rates their visit"}
::

The link opens a page, with no sign-in, asking "<name>, ¿qué tal te hemos atendido el <day>?" (how did we look after you on <day>?) with five buttons, from **Muy mal** (very bad) to **Muy bien** (very good). The page is always in Spanish.

- **With a 4 or 5**: "¡Nos alegra mucho! Gracias por tu valoración." (We're so glad! Thank you.) If your clinic has set up its review link, a **Dejar reseña en Google** (leave a Google review) button appears.
- **With a 1, 2 or 3**: "Sentimos que no haya ido bien. ¿Qué podemos mejorar?" (Sorry it didn't go well. What can we improve?). They can write a comment (optional, read directly by the clinic's management) and tap **Enviar** (send). They'll see a thank-you message.

Low scores appear under **Growth › Reputation** for the clinic to follow up and mark as resolved, and each clinic owner gets a task in **My Day** that same day, linked to the patient.

> **Good to know:** after a while the answer is locked and the page shows "Esta respuesta ya no se puede cambiar." (this answer can no longer be changed). An invalid link shows "Este enlace no es válido."
