If a patient arrives without having filled in their consent or intake form, there's no need for paper: with the staff app on a phone or tablet, you pick the forms and hand over the device. The patient only sees those forms, one after another, and can't leave them without the reception code or Face ID.

This is in the QuiroFlow staff app (iPhone, iPad or Android), not on the web.

## Prepare the forms

1. In the staff app, open the patient's record and tap **Sign at reception**.
2. Pick the forms: the ones **Waiting for them** and, if one is missing, **Add a form** from your templates.
3. The first time on that device, create a 4-to-6-digit **Reception code for this device** and enter it **Again**. It's the code for whoever takes the device back; it's asked for once on that device.
4. Tap **Hand it over · N forms** and give them the device.

> **Good to know:** form templates are created on the web, in **Settings › Docs**. If there are no pending forms and no templates, the app tells you there's nothing to sign. See [Forms, consents and letters](/articles/patients/patient-documents).

## What the patient does

The patient sees **Reception mode · Step 1 of N · Hello, <name>**. They fill in each form, sign, and tap **Sign and continue** (or **Sign and finish** on the last one). If something required is missing, the app tells them what. When they're done they see "All done. Please hand the device back to reception."

What they sign is saved under **Attachments** › **Documents** on their record as **Completed**, just as if they'd signed it from the link.

## Take the device back

Tap **Reception: take it back** and unlock it with Face ID or the reception code. If you've forgotten the code, you can use your staff account's password. **Leave reception mode** takes you back to the clinic's app.

> **Good to know:** so the patient can't leave the app either, turn on **Guided Access** on the iPhone or iPad (triple-click the side button) before handing it over.
