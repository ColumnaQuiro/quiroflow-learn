The patient portal is the patient app in a browser, for people who'd rather not install anything or prefer a computer. It shows the same as the app: their appointments, their plan, their payments, their exercises, the documents you share with them and their messages with the clinic. There's nothing to switch on: it's available to all your patients.

## How the patient signs in

::help-video{clip="portal--login" caption="A patient signs in to the portal"}
::

1. The patient goes to QuiroFlow's sign-in page and chooses the patient option (**Patient sign in**).
2. The first time, they enter your **clinic code** (the same as the app's; you'll find it in **Settings › Mobile App**). After that it isn't needed.
3. If they don't have an account yet, they click **Create your patient account**, enter their email and a password and click **Create account**. A confirmation link is sent to them (**Check your email**); if it doesn't arrive, they can click **Resend link** after a few seconds.
4. With an account, they enter their **Email** and **Password** and click **Sign in**. If they've forgotten it, **Forgot your password?**.

> **Good to know:** the account has to use **the same email that's on their record**; that's how QuiroFlow finds their record. If a staff member tries to sign in here, the portal tells them it's a staff account.

## What the patient sees

::help-video{clip="portal--tour" caption="A tour of the patient portal"}
::

- **Home**: "Hi, <name>", **Your next visit** (with **I'm here** on the day, confirm attendance, add to calendar and directions), their care plan, bono **Sessions left**, **Your credit** or what they owe (**You owe**), readings **For you**, the waitlist, **Message the clinic**, **Your exercises**, invite a friend and the clinic's details. With nothing booked, a **Book a visit** button (if you have online booking on) or a note to contact the clinic.
- **Appointments**: **Upcoming**, with **Cancel** when allowed (otherwise "Contact the clinic to change this one."), and **Past visits**.
- **Progress**: **Your progress**, with the posture assessments you've shared. See [Posture screen](/articles/clinical/posture-screen).
- **Billing**: **Credit**, **Outstanding**, **Your packages** (with the button to add them to their phone's wallet; see [Bonos in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet](/articles/patient-app/wallet-passes)), their membership, **Your facturas** as PDFs, and **Charges**, with **Pay** to pay what they owe online.
- **Exercises**: their home exercises, to tick off each day. See [Home exercises](/articles/clinical/exercises).
- **Documents**: the files you've shared with them (those set to **Custom** under **Attachments** › **Files**). See [Patient files and photos](/articles/patients/patient-files).
- **Messages**: "Write to your clinic and see their replies here." What they write reaches the clinic's **Inbox**.

> **Good to know:** forms to sign (consents, intake history) don't appear under **Documents**: they reach the patient only by link. See [Forms, consents and letters](/articles/patients/patient-documents).

## Checking in

On the day of the appointment, from 90 minutes before, the patient can click **I'm here** on **Home** (or open the check-in page). They see their arrival is recorded and are asked **How much pain do you have today?** from 0 to 10; their answer shows in **Progress** and in **My Day** as pain given by the patient on arrival.

If the clinic has an arrival tablet, **Show my code for the tablet** lets the patient show a QR code to identify themselves. See [Check-in](/articles/calendar-appointments/check-in).
