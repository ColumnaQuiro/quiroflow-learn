Your patients can install the QuiroFlow app on their phone (iPhone or Android) to see their appointments, book or move a visit, check in, pay what they owe, follow their care plan, do their exercises, read what you send them and message the clinic. No integration is needed: your clinic's code is all it takes.

The settings are in **Settings › Mobile App** (you need the **Clinic and diary** permission).

## Get your patients on the app

::help-video{clip="portal--app-settings" caption="The clinic code, the signs and the app links"}
::

**Share the app** has everything you need:

- **Clinic code**, with **Copy code**. The patient installs QuiroFlow, taps **Join your clinic** and enters this code once. The web portal asks for the same code. It's the same for the whole clinic, so it's fine to display it.
- **Print a desk sign**, **Print a check-in sign** and **Download QR** (the patient scans it from the join screen).
- **Store links, to send to a patient**, with **Copy**.
- **How it looks to a patient**: a preview.

You can also invite a patient from their record:

- **More actions** (⋯) › **Send the app on WhatsApp**: sends them the app links and the code on WhatsApp. It needs an approved WhatsApp template for it; if you don't have one, the dialog explains how to create it.
- **Copy invite**, next to the app status in the banner: copies a ready-to-paste text for a chat.

The record's banner tells you where they are: **No app**, **Has an app account**, or when they last opened it (for example "App · iPhone · opened 2d ago").

> **Good to know:** the patient must create their account with **the same email that's on their record**: that's how QuiroFlow finds their record. If the record has no email, add one first. A minor's app is used by their tutor.

## What patients can do

Under **What patients can do** you decide what's allowed from the app (only for patients who already have a record with you and have signed in):

- **Request a new appointment**. Only clinics, services and practitioners bookable under **Online Booking** are offered.
- **Cancel an appointment**.
- **Move an appointment to another time**.
- **Notice required to cancel or move**, in hours (0 to 336). Inside that window, the app asks them to contact the clinic.

Click **Save**.

## Send everyone an announcement

**Send an announcement** sends a notification to the phone of every patient with the app who hasn't opted out: a bank-holiday closure, a change of hours.

::help-video{clip="portal--app-announcement" caption="Preparing an announcement for patients with the app"}
::

1. Write the **Title** and the **Message**. On the right you see how it will look.
2. Click **Send to N patient(s)** and confirm with **Send now**.

> **Good to know:** a sent announcement can't be recalled. **Recently sent** lists the latest ones.

## How many use it

At the top of the page you see **Devices installed**, **Active in the last 30 days** and the split **By platform**. It counts devices, not patients (someone with two phones counts twice), and only counts app launches, not every return from the background.

## What the patient sees in the app

1. **Joining**: they tap **Join your clinic**, enter the code and **Continue**. Then they sign in or tap **Create your account** (with the email the clinic has) and confirm the email they receive. They can switch on Face ID to sign in.
2. **Home**: their next appointment (with check-in, confirm attendance, add to calendar, directions and change appointment), what they owe with a **Pay** button, their bono sessions, their credit, their care plan, **Your exercises**, readings **For you**, the waitlist, invite a friend, and the clinic's details.
3. At the bottom, the **Home**, **Plan** (next appointment, care plan, bonos and balance, visit history and invoices), **Progress**, **Documents** and **Messages** tabs.
4. **Booking** (if you allow it): clinic, appointment type, practitioner, day and time, an optional note and **Confirm booking**. Appointment types paid in advance online can't be booked from the app yet.
5. Under **Account**: language, appearance (light or dark), Face ID, the exercises **Daily reminder**, sign out and delete their account.

The same, from a browser, is in the patient portal. See [The patient portal](/articles/patient-app/patient-portal).

> **Good to know:** messages the patient writes from the app reach the clinic's **Inbox**, and your replies reach them in the app. See [The shared inbox](/articles/communications/the-shared-inbox).
