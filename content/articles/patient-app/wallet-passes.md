A patient with a bono can save it to Apple Wallet (iPhone) or Google Wallet (Android) as one more card on their phone. The card shows how many sessions they have left and their next visit, updates itself every time they use a session, and carries a QR code to show at the desk.

There's nothing to set up at the clinic: the card carries your clinic's name and logo.

## How the patient saves it

::help-video{clip="portal--wallet" caption="The button to save the bono to the wallet, in the portal"}
::

1. In the app, under **Plan** › sessions and balance, or in the portal, under **Billing** › **Your packages**, the patient finds their bono.
2. They tap **Add to Apple Wallet** or **Add to Google Wallet**. Only the one that matches their phone appears.
3. They confirm on the wallet's screen.

## What the card shows

- The sessions they have left (**QUEDAN**).
- Their next visit (**PRÓXIMA VISITA**).
- A QR code with the text "Enséñalo en recepción" (show it at the desk).

When they use a session or their next visit changes, the card updates itself and the phone can notify them ("Te quedan N sesiones en tu bono" — you have N sessions left on your bono). The card's text is in Spanish.

## At the desk

The card's QR code leads to the patient's record. If you scan it with **Scan** on the staff app's patient list and they have a visit today, that visit opens ready to take the session off the bono; otherwise their record opens.

To sell and manage bonos, see [Selling a bono](/articles/billing-payments/selling-a-package-bono).
