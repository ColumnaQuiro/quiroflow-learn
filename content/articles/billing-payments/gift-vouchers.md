With gift vouchers, anyone can buy an amount, a service or a bono on your public page to give to someone else. They're paid by card through your Stripe and arrive by email with a code. The person who receives it redeems it when booking online or at the clinic, from their **Money** tab.

To sell them online you need Stripe connected under **Settings › Payments** ([Payment methods and Stripe](/articles/settings/connecting-stripe)). If it isn't, the page says "Connect Stripe in Settings › Payments to sell them online."

## Setting up gift vouchers

::help-video{clip="billing--gift-settings" caption="Turning on online gift vouchers and choosing what is sold"}
::

1. Go to **Settings › Gift vouchers**.
2. Turn on **Sell gift vouchers online**. Below it you'll see **Your page:** with the link you can share.
3. Under **Amounts**, add the amounts people can give (enter one under **Amount in euros** and click **Add**). Optionally, tick **Allow another amount (20 to 300 €)**.
4. Under **Services and bonos**, tick the appointment types and bonos that can be given.
5. Under **Valid for**, choose how many months it lasts (6, 12, 18 or 24).
6. Click **Save**.

When redeemed, an amount or a service becomes credit on the patient's account (a service, for its price), and a bono becomes that bono.

> **Good to know:** the factura is issued **when the voucher is redeemed**, not when it is bought: as an amount on account, or as the bono it becomes. Check with your accountant (gestoría) that this suits a voucher for one particular service.

## What the buyer sees

On your gift page, the buyer:

1. Chooses an amount, or a service or bono.
2. Enters who it's for, who it's from and, optionally, a message.
3. Chooses how it arrives: by email to the recipient (with their email and, optionally, a delivery date) or to themselves, to hand over in person.
4. Enters their own email for the receipt and pays by card.

When done they see a confirmation, and the voucher is sent on the chosen date. The public gift page is shown in Spanish.

## Seeing the vouchers sold

In **Settings › Gift vouchers**, under **Sold**, each voucher is listed with its **Code**, what it is, **For**, **From** and its **Status**: **Not redeemed**, **Redeemed**, **Expired**, **Not sent yet** or the date it will go out. Click **Resend** if someone didn't receive it, or **Send now** to skip the scheduled date.

## Redeeming a gift voucher at the clinic

::help-video{clip="billing--gift-redeem" caption="Redeeming a gift voucher from the patient's record"}
::

1. On the patient's **Money** tab, under **On account**, click **Redeem gift voucher**.
2. Enter the code under **Gift voucher code** (it starts with REGALO-).
3. Click **Redeem**.

You'll see "Redeemed: … added as credit" or "Redeemed: … added to their bonos". The factura is issued at that moment.

> **Good to know:** it can also be redeemed when booking online, on your booking page ([Online booking](/articles/online-booking/setting-up-online-booking)).
