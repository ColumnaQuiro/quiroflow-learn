Packages -- also called *bonos* -- let a patient prepay for a set number of sessions at a fixed price. Set up your package templates first in **Settings &rarr; Packages**.

## Selling one

On a patient's **Billing** tab, find the **Packages / bonos** section:

1. Choose the package from the **Sell a package…** dropdown -- it shows the session count and price.
2. Enter **Paid now (€)** -- the amount actually collected today. This can be less than the full price if the patient is paying in installments.
3. If paying now, choose the **Method**: Cash, Card, or Credit on account (only shown if the patient has account credit available).
4. Select **Sell**.

The package appears in the patient's list showing sessions used and remaining, and can be logged against future visits.

## Partial payments and autopay

If the amount paid is less than the full price, the remaining balance stays outstanding. Adding a card to the patient (see [Adding a patient's card](/articles/billing-payments/adding-a-patients-card)) lets you enable autopay for what's left, so QuiroFlow charges it automatically on a schedule instead of chasing it manually.
