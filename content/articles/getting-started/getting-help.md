When you're not sure how to do something, you don't have to leave QuiroFlow. The **Support** button in the top bar opens a panel with two tabs: **Assistant**, which answers straight away from the articles in this help centre, and **QuiroFlow team**, for writing to a person on the QuiroFlow team.

## Asking the assistant

::help-video{clip="setup--help-assistant" caption="Asking the assistant a question"}
::

1. Click **Support** in the top bar.
2. Under **Assistant**, type your question into **Ask a question…**, in your own words: "How do I invite a receptionist?", "Where do I change the price of a first visit?".
3. Click **Ask**. While it searches you'll see **Looking that up…**.
4. The answer lists the articles it's based on underneath. Click one to read it in full.

The assistant only knows what these articles explain. It can't see your clinic's data (your patients, appointments or invoices) or change anything for you: it tells you where to do it.

## Writing to the QuiroFlow team

If the assistant can't find the answer, it offers **Ask a real person**. Click it and your question moves into the message box on the **QuiroFlow team** tab; you can also open that tab directly.

1. Type into **Message the QuiroFlow team…**.
2. Click **Send**.
3. We reply in this same panel during working hours. When there's an unread reply, you'll see it marked.

> **Good to know:** the conversation with the QuiroFlow team belongs to the clinic, not to one person: your colleagues see the questions you've asked and can follow them up.

> **Good to know:** each person has a number of assistant questions per hour and per day, more than enough for normal use. If you reach the limit, write to the QuiroFlow team.

## Other ways to reach us

You can also email **hola@quiroflow.com**. A person answers, in English or Spanish.
