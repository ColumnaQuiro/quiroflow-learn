Go to **Settings &rarr; Team** to invite staff members.

## Sending an invite

1. Enter the person's email address.
2. Choose a role from the dropdown.
3. Select **Create Invite Link**.

![Team Members settings page, with an invite form and Alex Rivera listed as Owner](/screenshots/settings-team.png)

QuiroFlow emails them a sign-up link. You'll also see the invite link on screen, in case you'd rather send it yourself (over WhatsApp, for example) instead of waiting on email delivery.

Once they click the link, create a password, and confirm their email, they're added to your team with the role you picked.

## Choosing a role

Every account starts with three built-in roles:

- **Owner** -- full access to everything: billing, team management, settings, and every patient and clinic. There's always exactly one Owner per account (whoever created it).
- **Practitioner** -- sees their own calendar and their own patients by default. Can manage visit notes and check patients in and out.
- **Front Desk** -- sees the whole calendar and every patient, can take payments and manage billing, but doesn't have access to account-wide settings.

If none of the built-in roles fit exactly, you can create a custom role with its own set of permissions from **Settings &rarr; Roles** -- see [Roles & permissions](/articles/team-roles/roles-and-permissions) for details.

## If someone's sidebar or Calendar looks wrong after accepting an invite

Refreshing the page usually fixes it -- a team member's permissions and calendar data load right after they first sign in, and very occasionally that first load finishes before everything is ready. If it's still wrong after a refresh, double check their role in **Settings &rarr; Team** matches what you intended.
