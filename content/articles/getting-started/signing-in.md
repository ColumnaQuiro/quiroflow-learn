The whole team signs in through the same door: **app.quiroflow.com/login**, with their email and password. If you or your clinic have turned on two-factor authentication, QuiroFlow asks for a code from your phone after the password. This article covers both, and what to do when something goes wrong.

## Signing in

::help-video{clip="setup--sign-in" caption="Signing in with email and password"}
::

1. Open **app.quiroflow.com/login**. At the top, leave **Clinic team** selected (the other side, **I'm a patient**, is how patients reach their portal).
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign in**. You land on your **Dashboard** (or the first page your role can see).

The language of the sign-in screen can be switched at the top right.

## If you've forgotten your password

::help-video{clip="setup--forgot-password" caption="Requesting a link and choosing a new password"}
::

1. On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot your password?**.
2. On **Reset your password**, enter your **Email** and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email you receive and click the link.
4. On **Set a new password**, type it into **New password** and again into **Confirm new password**.
5. Click **Update password**. You're taken straight to your dashboard.

> **Good to know:** the new password needs at least 8 characters and can't be one that has appeared in known breaches. If the link no longer works (**This link is invalid or has expired.**), click **Request a new one**.

> **Good to know:** an owner, or whoever manages the team, can also send you the link from **Settings → Team**, on your page (**Forgot their password? Send them an email to set a new one.** › **Send reset email**). Your old password keeps working until you choose the new one.

## Signing in with two-factor authentication

::help-video{clip="setup--two-factor-code" caption="Entering the authenticator code at sign-in"}
::

If you have two-factor authentication on, after clicking **Sign in** you'll see **Two-factor authentication**:

1. Open your authenticator app on your phone and find the QuiroFlow code.
2. Type the 6 digits into **Authentication code**. It checks itself once you reach the sixth; otherwise click **Verify**.

If your clinic requires two-factor and you don't have it yet, the screen reads **Set up two-factor authentication**: it has to be set up once before you can carry on. The steps are the same as in [Two-factor authentication](/articles/security-privacy/two-factor-login). If you can't do it right then, the only way out is **Sign out**.

## If you've lost your phone

Without your phone you can't generate the code. Ask an owner of your clinic, or whoever manages the team, to **reset your two-factor authentication** from **Settings → Team**, on your page. Next time you sign in you can set it up on the new phone.

If it's the only owner who lost their phone and nobody else can reset it, write to **hola@quiroflow.com** from the account's email address and we'll help you get back in.

## Signing in from your phone

The team can also use QuiroFlow from the iPhone and Android app, with the same email and password. See [The QuiroFlow app for your team](/articles/getting-started/staff-mobile-app).

> **Good to know:** if you're a patient looking for your portal, this isn't the way in: click **I'm a patient** or use the link your clinic gave you. See [The patient portal](/articles/patient-app/patient-portal).
