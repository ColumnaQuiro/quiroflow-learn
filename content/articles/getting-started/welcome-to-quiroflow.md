QuiroFlow is the day-to-day system for running your clinic: booking and managing appointments, keeping patient records, handling billing and payments, and communicating with patients over WhatsApp and email -- all in one place.

## The main areas

- **Calendar** -- book, confirm, and manage appointments across your clinics, rooms, and practitioners.
- **Patients** -- every patient's record: contact details, visit history, documents, and billing.
- **Recalls** -- a queue of patients who haven't booked in a while, so nobody falls through the cracks.
- **Inbox** -- every WhatsApp conversation with your patients, in one shared place your team can see.
- **Billing** -- packages (bonos), memberships, invoices, and payments, including cards patients have on file.
- **Campaigns** -- WhatsApp and email marketing to your patient list.
- **Reports** -- financial and activity reporting for your clinic.
- **Settings** -- your team, roles and permissions, clinics, and payment setup.

## Where to go next

If you're setting up QuiroFlow for the first time, start with [Creating your clinic](/articles/getting-started/creating-your-clinic) and [Inviting your team](/articles/getting-started/inviting-your-team).
