The **QuiroFlow** app for iPhone, iPad and Android isn't only for patients: your clinic's team uses it too. With your usual email and password you see your day, the calendar, records and the inbox, write visit notes from your phone and get notified when a patient arrives or an online booking comes in. It's the same app your patients download; signing in as staff shows the staff side.

The app's labels below are the English ones; with the app in Spanish they read as in the Spanish version of this article.

## Installing the app and signing in

1. Search for **QuiroFlow** in the App Store or Google Play and install it.
2. Open it and, on the sign-in screen, choose **Clinic team** (not **I'm a patient**).
3. Enter your **Email** and **Password**, the same ones you use on the computer, and tap **Sign in**.
4. If you have two-factor authentication, type the code from your authenticator app. If your clinic requires it and you don't have it yet, the app lets you set it up right there, with **Add to authenticator app on this phone**.
5. The app offers **Use Face ID to get in?** (or your fingerprint on Android). With **Turn on Face ID**, when you open QuiroFlow after more than two hours it asks for Face ID instead of your password. **Not now** leaves it for later, in your profile.

> **Good to know:** staff never need a clinic code to sign in; that's only for patients. If a patient had signed in on that phone before, signing in as staff switches the app to the staff side.

## What's in the app

At the bottom are the **My Day**, **Calendar**, **Patients**, **Inbox** (if your role has inbox access) and **Team** (team chat) tabs. On an iPad they're in a side menu.

- **My Day**: your visits today and what's pending: checking patients in, opening the visit to write the note, the patient's record, tasks, birthdays, recalls, the waitlist, overdue plans and payments. From here you also reach your clock-in (**My working time**) and, if your role allows, the **Cash shift**. **Privacy mode: names as initials** hides full names, in case someone looks at the screen.
- **Calendar**: the calendar, to book or move appointments.
- **Patients**: find a patient, create a new one or open their record, visits and notes.
- **Inbox**: patients' messages, as on the computer.
- **Team**: the internal chat, with a whole-team thread and one-to-one conversations.
- **Help › Alert the team**: sends an alert the whole team sees on screen and on their phones until someone answers.

On an iPad, **Sign at reception** turns the tablet into a station where the patient signs their forms. See [Documents and consents](/articles/patients/patient-documents).

## Your profile and notifications

On an iPhone or Android phone you reach your **Profile** from your photo on **My Day**. There you can:

- Switch **Clinic**, if you work at several: My Day, the calendar and new appointments use the one you choose.
- Choose your **Notifications**: **New online bookings**, **Cancellations and changes**, **Inbox messages**, **A patient checking in**, **Patients paying online or joining the waitlist**, **Recalls assigned to you**, **Summary of the day at 8:00** and **Weekly summary on Monday (also by email)**. **Do not disturb · Outside my working hours** stops everything outside your hours except the 8:00 summary.
- Change your password, two-factor and Face ID, the language and the appearance.
- **Sign out**. Signing out also makes that phone forget Face ID.

> **Good to know:** **Delete account** in the app is the same as **Remove my access** on the computer: it removes your login, not the clinic's data. See [Your account](/articles/getting-started/account-settings).

> **Good to know:** app releases go through Apple and Google review before they're published, so a label may sometimes trail the web by a version. Keep the app up to date.
