The first time you open your **Dashboard**, QuiroFlow shows the **Your first visit, three steps away** card. These are the three things the calendar needs to work: something to book, someone to book it for, and the appointment itself. Each step ticks itself off once it's done, whether you do it from the card or from anywhere else. At the end of this article there's a fourth step the card doesn't ask for but that's worth doing on day one: your clinic's opening hours.

## 1. Add what you offer

::help-video{clip="setup--first-steps-type" caption="Creating the first appointment type from the dashboard"}
::

An appointment type is what gets booked: "First visit", "Adjustment", "Review"… Each has its own length and price.

1. On the card, click **Add a type**. **New appointment type** opens.
2. Enter the **Name**, **Duration (min)** and **Price (€)**.
3. If patients should be able to book it online straight away, turn on **Bookable online from now**. Otherwise, switch it on later.
4. Click **Create and complete**. The type's page opens, where you can choose its colour and treatment stage. When you go back to the **Dashboard**, the step shows **Done**.

Create the other types you use the same way, from **Settings → Appointment Types**. See [Appointment types and services](/articles/settings/appointment-types-and-services).

## 2. Add your first patient

::help-video{clip="setup--first-steps-patient" caption="Adding the first patient"}
::

1. On the card, click **New patient**. **Add Patient** opens.
2. A **First name**, **Last name** and **Phone** are enough to start. Fill in the rest on their record later.
3. Click **Add Patient**.

Coming from another system? Instead of adding patients one by one, click **Coming from PracticeHub? Import** or go to **Settings → Import**: you can bring them over from PracticeHub or from a CSV file. See [Importing your data](/articles/settings/importing-your-data).

## 3. Book their first visit

::help-video{clip="setup--first-steps-book" caption="Opening the calendar to book the first visit"}
::

1. On the card, click **Open the calendar**. The calendar opens with your newest patient already chosen.
2. Click a free slot, choose the appointment type and confirm the booking.
3. Back on the **Dashboard**, the card says **Your first visit is booked**.

By default, QuiroFlow sends the patient a reminder 24 hours before; you decide how and when in [Confirmations and reminders](/articles/communications/confirmations-and-reminders). For everything else about booking, see [Booking an appointment](/articles/calendar-appointments/booking-an-appointment).

## 4. Set your clinic's opening hours

::help-video{clip="settings--clinic-hours" caption="Setting the clinic's opening hours"}
::

A new clinic starts with no opening hours. Until it has some, the calendar marks no time as closed and online booking can only offer practitioners who have hours of their own. Set them before you share your booking page:

1. Go to **Settings → Clinics** and open your clinic.
2. Under **Hours**, switch on the days you open and set the hours for each. **+ Range** adds a second range on the same day (morning and afternoon), and **Copy Monday to Tuesday–Friday** saves you repeating it.
3. Click **Save changes**.

These are the default hours for every practitioner. If someone works fewer days, give them their own hours: see [A practitioner's working hours](/articles/team-roles/practitioner-working-hours). For holidays and closures, see [Holidays and closures](/articles/settings/holidays-and-closures).
