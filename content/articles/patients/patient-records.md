## Adding a patient

The fastest way is right from the Calendar when booking an appointment -- search for them, and if they're not found, add them without leaving the booking panel (first name, last name, email, phone).

You can also go to **Patients** and add one directly, with more fields available (date of birth, national ID, address, referral source, preferred language, and more).

## The patient page

Open any patient to see their full record, organized into tabs:

- **Overview** -- contact details, flags, care plan, and account summary at a glance.
- **Appointments** -- their full visit history and any upcoming bookings.
- **Visit notes** -- clinical notes recorded by practitioners.
- **Billing** -- invoices, payments, packages, memberships, and cards on file. See the **Billing & Payments** collection.
- **Communications** -- every message sent to or from this patient.
- **Docs** -- forms and documents sent for them to fill out or sign.
- **Files** -- uploaded files (photos, reports, etc).

## Flags

The **Flags** card on Overview surfaces anything staff should know at a glance -- things like do-not-contact, minor status, or other account markers. Select **Edit** to change them.
