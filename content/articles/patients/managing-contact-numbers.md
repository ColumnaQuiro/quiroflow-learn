A patient can have more than one phone number on file. Find them on the patient's **Overview** tab under **Contact**.

## Adding or editing a number

Select the phone number field to edit it directly -- no need to delete and re-add it to fix a typo or update it.

![Contact card showing a phone number with a WhatsApp badge](/screenshots/patient-overview.png)

## WhatsApp

Each number has a **WhatsApp** checkbox. Whichever number has it checked is the one QuiroFlow sends confirmations, reminders, and campaign messages to, and where inbound replies get matched back to this patient. If a patient has multiple numbers, make sure the right one is flagged -- only one should be marked as WhatsApp-enabled at a time.
