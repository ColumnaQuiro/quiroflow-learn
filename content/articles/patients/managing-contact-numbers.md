A patient can have several phone numbers (mobile, work, home). What matters is marking which one has WhatsApp, because that's the number QuiroFlow sends confirmations, reminders and messages to, and the number it recognises their replies by in the inbox. In the same place you decide how they prefer to be reminded and, if they ask, that they're not contacted at all.

All of this is on the patient record: **Overview** tab › **Edit all details**.

## Add or change a phone number

::help-video{clip="patients--phones" caption="Adding a second number and marking the WhatsApp one"}
::

1. Open the record and click **Edit all details**.
2. Under **Phone numbers**, each number has its own row: country prefix, number, the **WhatsApp** checkbox and an ✕ to remove it.
3. To add one, type it in the empty row and click **Add**.
4. Tick **WhatsApp** on the number that has it, and untick it on the ones that don't.

Phone numbers save on their own, straight away, with no need to click **Save**. If one can't be saved, you'll see **Not saved** next to that row with the reason.

> **Good to know:** the phone shown in the record's banner is the first one added, which isn't necessarily the WhatsApp one. Messages go to the number marked **WhatsApp**; if none is marked, the first number on the record is tried. Keep just one ticked, the one they actually use.

## Communication preferences

In the same dialog, the **Communication preferences** section decides how this particular patient is contacted:

- **Marketing channels**: which channels they accept campaigns and promotions on.
- **Reminder type** and **Confirmation type**: the channel their appointment reminders and confirmations go by.
- **Appointment reminder**: how far ahead they're reminded. By default it follows the clinic (**Clinic default**), but you can pick another, such as **1 day before**.
- **Email receipt when an appointment is processed**: emails them the receipt each time a visit is charged.

Click **Save** when you're done.

> **Good to know:** if reminders are switched off for the whole clinic, you'll see a notice: none is sent until they're switched on in **Settings › Messages**. See [Confirmations and reminders](/articles/communications/confirmations-and-reminders).

## Do not contact

If a patient asks you not to message them, mark them **Do not contact** rather than relying on the team to remember.

::help-video{clip="patients--do-not-contact" caption="Marking a patient Do not contact"}
::

1. Click **Edit all details**.
2. Under **Under age & do not contact**, tick **Do not contact (blocks all communications and recalls)**.
3. Click **Save**.

The banner shows the red **Do not contact** pill and the **Message** button disappears. From then on nothing is sent to them: no reminders, confirmations, campaigns or automations, and their visit history can't be sent either. To contact them again, untick the box and save.

> **Good to know:** a patient who is under age doesn't receive messages directly either; messages about their appointments go to their tutor. See [Minors, tutors and family](/articles/patients/minors-and-family).
