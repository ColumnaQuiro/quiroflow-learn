Beyond the staff-facing web app your team uses, QuiroFlow has a companion app patients can install on their own phone. Setting it up for your clinic takes one code, not a technical integration.

![Mobile App settings page with the clinic's join code, QR code, and app usage stats](/screenshots/settings-mobile-app.png)

## The join code

Go to **Settings -> Mobile App** to find your clinic's code. A patient downloads the QuiroFlow app, taps **Join your clinic**, and enters this code once (or scans the QR code, which encodes the same thing) -- after that, their account is linked to your clinic and they don't need the code again.

Share it however makes sense for your front desk: a printed QR code on the counter, in a welcome email or SMS, on a printed receipt, or read aloud over the phone. There's nothing patient-specific about the code -- it's the same one for your whole clinic, so it's safe to display publicly.

## App usage

The same settings page shows how many devices have the app installed and how many were active in the last 30 days, broken down by iOS, Android, and web. Two things worth knowing about how this is counted:

- It counts **devices**, not patient accounts -- one patient using the app on both a phone and a tablet counts as two.
- It counts **app launches**, not every time the app is reopened from the background -- so this reflects genuine usage rather than the app simply sitting open on someone's phone.

If usage stays near zero after you've shared the code, that's a sign the code isn't reaching patients yet (worth checking it's actually on your receipts or welcome messages) rather than a sign patients aren't interested.
