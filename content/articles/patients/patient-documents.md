On a patient's **Docs** tab, you can create documents from a reusable template (built in **Settings &rarr; Document Templates**) or start a blank one.

## Sending a document

1. Select **New document**.
2. Choose a template from the list, or start blank.
3. Share it with the patient using **Open link** (opens it in a new tab yourself) or **Copy link** (to send however you like -- WhatsApp, email).

The patient fills it out on their own device, no account or login required -- just the link.

![A patient's Docs tab with a "New doc" button](/screenshots/patient-docs.png)

## Tracking status

Each document shows **Sent** until the patient submits it, then **Complete**. You can delete a document at any point if it was created by mistake or is no longer needed.
