**Attachments** › **Files** holds everything the clinic uploads to a patient's record: reports, imaging, X-rays, photos, or a PDF they brought in. From there you also decide which files the patient sees in their app and portal, and you can send them one on WhatsApp protected by a password.

## Upload a file

::help-video{clip="patients--upload-file" caption="Uploading a file and sharing it with the patient"}
::

1. On the record, open the **Attachments** tab and scroll down to **Files uploaded by the clinic**.
2. Click **Upload file**, or drag the files onto the box (**Drop files here, or click to choose**).
3. Each file appears under **Files uploaded by the clinic** with a thumbnail.

For each file you can:

- Click the name to **Rename** it.
- Tick **X-ray** (images only) so it can be compared under **Progress** › **Posture** › **X-rays**.
- Use **Preview** or **Download**.
- Delete it with the bin. It's removed from this patient for everyone and can't be recovered; you need the **Delete files** permission.

> **Good to know:** on upload, QuiroFlow compresses large photos and PDFs so they take up less space, with no visible difference on screen.

## Share a file with the patient

Each file has a selector with two options:

- **Generic**: only the clinic sees it. This is the default.
- **Custom**: the patient sees it too, under **Documents** in their app and portal.

Switch the report or exercise sheet you want them to have to hand to **Custom**. See [The patient portal](/articles/patient-app/patient-portal).

## Send a password-protected PDF on WhatsApp

To send a report on WhatsApp without just anyone being able to open it, send it as a password-protected PDF.

1. On the file (PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP), click the padlock **Send protected by WhatsApp**.
2. Under **Password**, QuiroFlow fills in their DNI/NIE if it's on file (**Their DNI/NIE on file.**). If it isn't, type it, or type another password you'll tell them in person or by phone. If you type an ID number, you can save it to their record.
3. Write a **Message**. It can't contain the password: tell them which password it is (for example "your ID number"), not what it is.
4. Click **Send**. You'll see **Sent to the patient on WhatsApp, password-protected.**

> **Good to know:** WhatsApp only allows free-form messages within 24 hours of the patient's last message. If it's been longer, ask them to message you first. It also can't be sent to a minor or to a patient marked **Do not contact**, and you need access to the inbox.

## Change the profile photo

::help-video{clip="patients--profile-photo" caption="Changing the profile photo with your phone"}
::

1. In the record's banner, click the photo or initials (**Change profile photo**).
2. Choose **Upload from computer**, or **Use your phone (QR)** to take the photo with your phone.
3. With the QR: scan it with your phone (**Scan with your phone**), tap **Abrir cámara**, take the photo and you'll see **¡Foto subida!**. On the computer, the photo appears on the record by itself.

The QR link doesn't ask anyone to sign in and only works for that patient's photo.

## Who sees what

- Viewing and uploading files comes with access to the record. With **Documents and files they see** set to **Only their own**, each person sees the files they uploaded (imported files with no author are visible to everyone).
- Every time someone opens or downloads a file, it's written to the **Activity log**.
- A file marked **Not migrated** is one imported from another system whose content never came across.
