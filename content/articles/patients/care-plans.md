A care plan tracks a patient's planned course of care -- for example, "10 visits, every 2 weeks" -- and shows progress right on their Overview tab.

## Creating one

On the patient's **Overview** tab, find the **Care Plan** card and select **Add plan** (or **Edit** if one already exists).

Give it a name, the total number of planned visits, and how often they're expected (e.g. every 1 or 2 weeks). QuiroFlow tracks completed visits against the plan automatically as appointments are marked complete, showing a simple progress bar like "3 of 10 visits."

![Care Plan card showing "Wellness Plan, 1 of 10 visits, every 2 weeks" with a progress bar](/screenshots/patient-overview.png)

## Why it's useful

A care plan is informational -- it doesn't block booking or billing -- but it's a quick way for both staff and practitioners to see whether a patient is on track, falling behind, or due to wrap up their course of treatment.
