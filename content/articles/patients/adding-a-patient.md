There are two ways to register a patient: from the **Patients** list, with all their details, or straight from the calendar while you book their first appointment. The second is quickest when someone rings for an appointment; the first, when you already have all their details to hand.

## From the patient list

::help-video{clip="patients--add-patient" caption="Adding a patient from the list"}
::

1. Go to **Patients** and click **New patient**.
2. Type the **First name** (the only field that is always asked for) and the **Last name**.
3. Fill in whatever you have: **Date of birth** (the age shows as you type), **Email**, **Phone**, **Sex**, **Referral source**, **Occupation**, **Preferred Language**, **Street address** (used on invoices), **Postal code**, **City**, **Country**, **Patient Note**, **Clinic** and **Tags** (separated by commas).
4. If the phone has WhatsApp, tick **This number has WhatsApp**: that is what lets confirmations and reminders go to them on WhatsApp.
5. Click **Add Patient**. Their record opens straight away.

Anything you leave out now can be filled in later from the record, with **Edit all details**. See [The patient record](/articles/patients/patient-records).

> **Good to know:** QuiroFlow doesn't check whether the patient already exists. Before creating someone, search the list by name, phone or email. If you still end up with two records for the same person, you can join them: see [Merging duplicate records](/articles/patients/merging-patients).

## From the calendar

When booking an appointment, search for the patient in the booking panel; if they don't come up, you can create them right there with the basics (name, last name, email, phone) without leaving the booking. See [Booking an appointment](/articles/calendar-appointments/booking-an-appointment).

## Which fields appear

Which fields the form shows, and which are required, is set by your clinic in **Settings › New Patient Fields**: each field can be **Hidden**, **Optional** or **Required**. Your clinic's own fields (for example "Sport they play") are created in **Settings › Custom Fields** and can also be asked for at registration. See [New patient fields and referral sources](/articles/settings/patient-intake-fields).

The **Referral source** options are your clinic's referral sources (Google, Instagram, Recommendation…), managed in **Settings › Referral Sources**.

> **Good to know:** if the phone or one of your own fields couldn't be saved, the patient is still created and you'll see a notice starting "Patient created, but…". Open their record and add what was missing.
