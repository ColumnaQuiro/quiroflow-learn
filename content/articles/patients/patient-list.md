The **Patients** list is where you find any of your patients. Besides searching by name, you can filter it (who owes money, who has no next visit, whose bono is almost used up…), export the result, and save any combination of filters as a **segment** the whole team can reopen in one click.

It's in the sidebar under **Patients › Patients**. The header shows how many patients there are and how many are shown ("62 patients · 50 shown"); the list shows 50 per page.

## Search and filter

::help-video{clip="patients--list-filter" caption="Searching for a patient and filtering the list"}
::

1. Type in the search box (**Name, phone, email or ID**). The list narrows as you type.
2. Use the drop-downs in the filter row:
   - **Status**: **Active**, **Inactive** or **Any status**.
   - **Care plan: any** / **On a care plan**.
   - **Balance: any** / **Owing** / **In credit**.
   - **Missing contact**: **Missing email** or **Missing phone**.
   - **Practitioner**.
3. Switch on **Do not contact** or **Minors** to see only those patients.
4. Click **More filters** to narrow it further: **Tags (all of)**, **Last visit** (from/to), **Patient since** (created from/to), **Next appointment** (**Has a future appointment** / **No future appointment**), **Bono** (**Has a live bono** / **No live bono**, plus **sessions left ≤**), **Patient app** (**Uses the app** / **Does not use the app**), **Clinic** and **Birthday this month**.
5. Each active filter shows as a chip above the list. Remove one with its ✕, or all of them with **Clear all**.

Each row shows the contact (marked **WA** when the number has WhatsApp), the last and next visit, the care plan, tags and balance. The **⋯** menu on each row has shortcuts to **Open record**, **Open billing** and **Open messages**.

> **Good to know:** the list starts on **Status: Active**. Archived patients only appear once you choose **Inactive** or **Any status**.

## Save a segment

A segment saves the **filters**, not a fixed list of people: each time you open it you see whoever matches those filters at that moment. It's ideal for lists you go through often, like "Bono almost used up" or "No future appointment".

::help-video{clip="patients--list-segment" caption="Saving the filters as a segment"}
::

1. Apply the filters you want.
2. Click **Save as segment**.
3. Type a **Name** (e.g. "Bono almost used up").
4. Leave **Share with the team** ticked so everyone sees it, or untick it to keep it to yourself (it then carries the **Private** label).
5. Click **Save**. The segment sits in the row at the top, next to **All patients**.

To change a segment, open it and adjust the filters: you'll see **Filters changed** and can choose **Save changes** or **Save as new segment**. You can also **Rename** or **Delete** a segment; deleting it only removes the saved filter, no patient is changed.

## Export the list

Click **Export** to download a CSV file of every patient matching the current filters (not just the page you can see): first name, last name, email, balance, status, under age, do not contact and tags.

> **Good to know:** every export is written to the clinic's **Activity log**, with who did it and how many rows it held. See [Activity log](/articles/security-privacy/activity-log).
