With the **Referral programme**, each patient gets their own link to your booking page, in the app and the patient portal. When someone books for the first time through that link, they're saved as referred by that person and your "patient referred" automations run (a thank-you message, for example). That way you know who brings you new patients and who you owe a thank-you.

It's switched on in **Settings › Clinic › Online Booking**. You need the **Open Settings** and **Clinic and diary** permissions.

## Switching it on

::help-video{clip="booking--referral-programme" caption="Switching on the referral programme and saying what they get"}
::

1. In **Settings › Clinic › Online Booking**, go to **Referral programme** and turn on the switch.
2. Under **What they get**, type the thank-you you give whoever refers someone (for example "A free session when your friend comes to their first visit"). It's shown to the patient beside their link.
3. Click **Save changes**.

## What the patient sees

The app and the portal show the **Invite a friend** card: "Share your link: when a friend books their first visit with it, we will know it was you.", with **Share** and **Copy link**, and the reward you wrote.

## Giving the reward

QuiroFlow records who brought whom, but you give the reward. **Reports › Referrals** lists each referral (**Referred by**, **Friend**, **Since**, **First visit**) and how many have a **Reward to give**. Once you've given it, click **Mark given** (or **Undo** if you got it wrong). See the [Reports](/articles/reports) collection.

> **Good to know:** you can also record by hand on a patient's record who referred them, with **Referred by**; it counts the same in the report.

> **Good to know:** to say thank you automatically, create an automation with the **Patient referred someone** trigger. See [Automations](/articles/communications/automations).
