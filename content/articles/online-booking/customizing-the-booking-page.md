**Settings &rarr; Online Booking** has several tabs beyond General for shaping the booking experience:

## Bookable Entities

For each appointment type, open its row to set:

- **Bookable by** -- everyone, new patients only, or existing patients only.
- **Bypass practitioner selection** -- skip straight to date/time instead of asking which practitioner.
- **Max days ahead override** -- a per-type limit, if different from the account-wide default in General.
- **Deposit amount** -- charge a fixed deposit instead of the full price, when online payment is required for that type.

![Bookable Entities tab listing appointment types with a Configure link for each](/screenshots/settings-online-booking-entities.png)

## Discount Codes

Create a code with either a percentage or fixed amount off, an optional expiry date, and a maximum number of uses. Patients enter it during checkout on the booking page.

## Layout

Set your primary and secondary colors and choose whether to show your clinic logo, so the booking page matches your branding.

## Language Overrides

The booking page's text (headings, button labels, step titles) can be overridden here if you want different wording than the defaults -- useful for translating it or matching your own tone.
