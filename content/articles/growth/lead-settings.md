In **Settings › Communication › Leads** you decide how enquiries move along Growth › Leads, what each is worth, and who on the team is told when one arrives. It's also where you connect the Facebook Page you run lead ads from, so every form reaches QuiroFlow within seconds.

You need Growth and the **Open Settings** and **Communication and Growth** permissions.

## Connecting your Facebook and Instagram lead ads

1. Under **Facebook & Instagram lead ads**, click **Connect**.
2. Sign in with Meta and choose the Facebook Page you run your ads from.
3. The Page appears in the list with its **Last lead** (or "No leads yet since connecting").

Each form submitted reaches **Leads** and starts your **New lead** automations (for example the **Ad lead** template, which answers within minutes). With several Pages, use **Add a Page**.

- **Fetch missed leads** checks Facebook for any form that's missing (from a time the connection was down, say) and adds it.
- **Disconnect** stops receiving forms from that Page.

> **Good to know:** the consent switch makes submitting the form count as consent to marketing messages, for forms without their own consent box. Turn it on only if your form's privacy text says so.

## The pipeline

::help-video{clip="growth--lead-settings" caption="Converting automatically after one visit and alerting the team by email"}
::

**The pipeline** shows the stages and whether each moves **Automatic** or **By hand**:

- **New**: when the enquiry arrives.
- **Contacted**: someone has spoken to them.
- **Qualified**: worth a first visit.
- **Booked**: an appointment is made, however it is booked. Matched by email or phone.
- **Showed**: checked in, or the visit completed.
- **Converted**: by hand, unless you turn on **Convert automatically**.

Leads only move forward, never out of **Lost**, and only for appointments on or after the day the lead came in. **Lost** is set by hand, from any step.

To have them move to **Converted** on their own:

1. Turn on **Convert automatically**.
2. Choose after how many **Attended visits**, and of which **Appointment type** (or "any appointment type").
3. Click **Save changes**.

## Default value

The **Default value** (in euros, or **None**) is used for leads without their own value: it counts in the board's total and in revenue attributed on the Overview. Leads from a Meta form never carry their own value.

## Telling the team

Under **Tell the team when a lead arrives**, enter an email (**By email**) and/or a number (**By WhatsApp**, in +34… format) so a person follows up. The instant reply to the lead comes from automations; this alert is for the team.

> **Good to know:** if that number hasn't written to the clinic in the last 24 hours, WhatsApp only delivers the **New lead alert (to staff)** template chosen in **Settings › Communication › WhatsApp**.

Click **Save changes** when you're done.
