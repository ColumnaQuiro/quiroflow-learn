A **lead** is someone who is interested in your clinic but isn't a patient yet: they filled in one of your ad forms, wrote on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or your website chat, or booked from an ad. **Growth › Leads** gathers them on a board by stage, so nobody goes unanswered and you can see where each one is.

You need Growth and the **Communication and Growth** permission.

## Where they come from

- Forms from your Facebook and Instagram ads, within seconds (see [Lead settings and Facebook lead ads](/articles/growth/lead-settings)).
- Anyone who writes on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or your website chat without being a patient.
- Online bookings that come from an ad.
- Other tools, through the public API.

## The board

At the top you see how many **open leads** there are, their estimated value and how many the AI is handling right now. You can switch between **Board** and **Table**, and search by name, phone or email.

The columns are the funnel stages: **New Lead**, **Contacted**, **Qualified**, **Booked**, **Showed**, **Converted** and **Lost** (in the Spanish interface they currently show with these English names too). Each card shows the name, the source, how long it has been in that stage and, if the receptionist is handling it, **AI handling**.

::help-video{clip="growth--leads-move-open" caption="Moving a lead to another stage and opening it"}
::

1. Drag a card to another column to change its stage.
2. Click a card to open it.
3. Under **Timeline** you see everything that has happened, newest last: the form they filled in, the conversation, the AI's qualification, the booked appointment (with **Open in calendar**).
4. Alongside are **Contact**, **Attribution** (the campaign and ad they came from) and **Consent**.

From there you can send a **Message** (opens their conversation in the Inbox), **Call** or **Book appointment**.

> **Good to know:** many stages move on their own: to contacted when someone talks to them, to booked when an appointment is made, to showed when they check in. Leads only move forward; lost is set by hand and they never leave it. The rules are in **Settings › Leads**.

## Estimated value

Each lead can have an **Estimated value** in euros (what you expect them to be worth as a patient). It feeds the board's total and the revenue attributed on the Overview. To change it, click the value on the lead, type the amount in **Estimated value in euros** and click **Save**. Leave it empty and the default from **Settings › Leads** is used, marked "(default)".

## Converting to a patient

When the lead books or comes in, convert them: their patient record is created and where they came from is kept.

::help-video{clip="growth--lead-convert" caption="Converting a lead into a patient and booking their first visit"}
::

1. Open the lead and click **Convert to patient**.
2. If QuiroFlow finds a record with the same phone or email, it asks "This person may already be a patient". Click **Link** to keep one record (a second would split their history and balance), or **Create a new record anyway**.
3. You'll see "… is now a patient · Book their first appointment now?". Click **Book appointment** to pick the time on the calendar, or **Not now, open record**.

Once converted, the button becomes **Open patient record**. You can also convert from the conversation in the [Inbox](/articles/communications/the-shared-inbox), with **Convert to patient**.

> **Good to know:** in **Settings › Leads** you can make conversion automatic after one or more visits.

## If the board is empty

With "No leads yet" you'll see a list of what's needed to start receiving them: publishing the booking page, connecting your WhatsApp Business number, turning on the AI receptionist and importing your Meta Ads lead form.
