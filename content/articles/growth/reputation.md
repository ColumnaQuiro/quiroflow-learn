**Growth › Reputation** brings together three things: your clinic's **Google reviews** (with an AI-drafted reply for each, which you approve), the **review requests** you send patients, and the **satisfaction survey** they get after a visit. It helps you answer in time, know what patients think and hear early about anything that didn't go well.

You need Growth and the **Communication and Growth** permission.

## Connecting your Google Business Profile

1. Under **Google reviews**, click **Connect Google**.
2. Sign in with the Google account that owns the clinic's profile.
3. If it manages several listings, QuiroFlow asks "Which listing is this clinic's?". Pick yours.
4. You'll see "Connected to …" with the imported reviews.

From then on, **Refresh** fetches new reviews and **Disconnect** removes the connection. At the top you see the **Average rating**, how many reviews you have on Google and the **Trend** (12 months, 2 years or all).

> **Good to know:** if your installation doesn't offer the Google connection yet, the section shows as "Coming soon".

## Replying to a review

A reply is drafted with AI for every new review. Those waiting are at the top, under **Needs approval**.

::help-video{clip="growth--reputation-approve" caption="Adjusting and approving the reply to a review"}
::

1. Read the review and the draft marked "Drafted by AI · needs your approval".
2. Click **Edit** to change anything.
3. Click **Approve**: the reply is published on Google under your clinic's name. Or **Discard** if you don't want to answer with that text.

Nothing posts without your approval. On a review without a draft, **Draft a reply with AI** prepares one. Answered reviews show as **Replied** or **Replied · AI drafted, approved**.

## Asking patients for reviews

Requests go out through an automation. The **Ask for a review** template writes to patients two days after their visit, only to those who had no incidents, with the link to your review page.

1. Put your review link in **Settings › Communication › Messages › Google review link**.
2. In **Automations**, click **Templates** and choose **Ask for a review**.
3. Check the message and switch it on.

Under **Review requests** you see how many were **Sent**, how many **Opened the link** and how many **Left a review**. Opening is counted because the link goes through QuiroFlow; whether they left a review can only be known with Google connected.

## The satisfaction survey

The **How did we do?** automation template asks the patient for a score from 1 to 5 a few hours after their first visit and then every so many visits (never more than once a month). Until you switch it on, the **Satisfaction** panel offers **Set it up in Automations**.

::help-video{clip="growth--satisfaction-survey" caption="What the patient sees when they open the survey"}
::

The patient opens the link without signing in and sees "¿Qué tal te hemos atendido el …?" ("How did we do on …?") with scores from 1 (Muy mal, very bad) to 5 (Muy bien, very good). The page is in Spanish:

- With 1, 2 or 3, it asks what could be improved and alerts the clinic's owners.
- With 4 or 5, it thanks them and, if you've set your review link, offers **Dejar reseña en Google** (leave a review on Google).

The **Satisfaction** panel shows the **Average** score for the last 30 days, how many **Answered**, the **Spread** of scores and the average **By practitioner**. **To look at** lists the scores of 1 to 3 with their comment (seen only by owners and managers): **Call** the patient and, once you've talked it through, **Mark resolved**.
