**Growth** is the part of QuiroFlow for winning new patients: it captures every enquiry that comes in through your ads, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or your website chat as a lead, follows it until they book and come in, and tells you which channel brings you patients and what each one costs. It includes the [AI receptionist](/articles/growth/ai-receptionist) and [Google reviews](/articles/growth/reputation).

In the menu it's the **Growth** group: **Overview**, **Leads**, **AI Receptionist** and **Reputation**. You need the **Communication and Growth** permission.

## Who has Growth

- The **Practice** and **Clinic** plans include it.
- On the **Solo** plan it can be added from **Subscription**. Until you have it, the Growth screens show a blurred example and an **Add Growth** button.
- The trial includes Growth.
- The AI receptionist can also be bought on its own, without Growth.

> **Good to know:** without Growth, leads from your Facebook and Instagram ads and from the API are not captured. It isn't just that you can't see them: they aren't stored. Lead automations stay paused until you have it again.

Automations (reminders, birthdays, winning patients back) are on every plan; see [Automations](/articles/communications/automations).

## Reading the Overview

**Growth › Overview** opens the **Growth dashboard** for the month to date, with all the account's clinics together.

- **New leads this month**, with those of the last 90 days underneath.
- **Cost per lead**, **Cost per new patient** and **ROAS**: they appear as soon as ad spend is recorded for this month.
- **Revenue attributed**: the estimated value of leads converted into patients. It's an estimate (the value you gave each lead, or the default in **Settings › Leads**), not what was invoiced.
- **Acquisition funnel**: how many leads reached each step, from new to converted, and where most drop off ("Biggest drop-off: N leads did not reach…"). **Review the pipeline** takes you to the leads board.
- **Channels**: for each source (Meta Ads, Google Ads, Instagram, Web…), the **Spend**, **Leads**, **Booked**, shows and **Cost per new patient**.
- **Needs attention**: leads waiting more than a week, AI replies waiting for approval, leads with no source, or no ad spend recorded.
- **AI receptionist**: how many conversations it wrote in this month and how many visits it booked or moved on its own.
- **Leads vs booked · last 90 days**: the trend.

## Recording what you spent on ads

For the cost per patient to be real, the dashboard needs to know what you spent on each channel this month.

::help-video{clip="growth--overview-spend" caption="Recording the month's Meta Ads spend"}
::

1. Under **Channels**, click the **Spend** cell for the channel (Meta Ads, say).
2. Type the month's amount and press Enter.
3. Cost per lead and per new patient are recalculated.

To have it filled in for you, connect **Meta Ads spend** in **Settings › Communication › WhatsApp** with your ad account ID and a read-only token. Nothing there can spend money.

> **Good to know:** the dashboard covers the month to date and can't be filtered by date. Leads with no source count in the funnel but in no channel. The full report is under **Reports**.
