The **AI receptionist** answers messages that reach the Inbox on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and your website chat, from new leads and from patients alike. It answers with what it knows about your clinic, asks the qualifying questions you give it, offers real free times from your calendar, and can book or move an appointment when the patient accepts. When something isn't for it, it hands over to a person on the team.

It's in **Growth › AI Receptionist**. It's included in Growth and can also be bought on its own. You need the **Communication and Growth** permission.

The page has three tabs: **Overview**, **What it knows** and **How it behaves**. On the right (or on the **Try it** tab on narrow screens) there's a chat to try it out.

## Getting it running

On **Overview**, the **Getting it ready** list tells you what's missing: at least one appointment type it may offer, with a price and a stage; telling it what isn't in Settings; trying it; connecting WhatsApp or Instagram; and turning it on.

Then choose **How it answers**:

::help-video{clip="growth--receptionist-mode" caption="Choosing how the receptionist answers"}
::

- **Off**: it reads nothing and writes nothing.
- **Drafts for you to approve**: it writes a reply to every enquiry and patient message; a person reads it in the Inbox and sends it, edits it or discards it. Nothing goes out on its own. The best way to start.
- **Answers on its own**: it sends its replies itself in the hours you choose, and books or moves visits when a patient accepts a time. In the other hours it drafts.

With **Answers on its own**, under **When it sends on its own** tick **Outside clinic hours (nights, weekends)** and, if you like, **During clinic hours too**. At the top, **Right now** sums up what it's doing at this moment.

**Channels** shows which channels it's connected to; **Set up** takes you to connect them.

## Teaching it what it knows

What's already in Settings (appointment types, prices, hours, clinics) it reads by itself. Under **What it knows** you tell it the rest.

::help-video{clip="growth--receptionist-knowledge" caption="Adding a card about the first visit"}
::

1. Go to **What it knows**.
2. Under **Clinic knowledge**, click one of the suggestions under **Start from:** (for example **The first visit**), or type your own **Title**.
3. Under **Facts, one per line**, fill in each ___ with your own details.
4. Click **Add card**.

On the same tab, under **Persona**, you choose the **Name it answers with**, the **Tone** (**Warm and brief**, **Clinical**, **Chatty** or **Formal (usted)**), the **Languages it answers in** and what it **Never says** (for example "Never quote a discount"). Under **Messages it does not answer** you list replies like "Gracias" or "Vale" that need no answer.

> **Good to know:** it takes prices from your appointment types and bonos, not from the cards. If a price changes, change it in Settings and it will know.

## How it behaves

Under **How it behaves**:

- **Qualification questions**: for example "How long have you had it?". It asks them in this order, one at a time, and only once it has dealt with what the patient asked.
- **When it hands over to a person**: **When…** / **Then…** rules, for example "they mention chest pain" → "hand to a chiropractor now". Click **Add rule**.
- **Booking rules**: **Offers times up to** N **days ahead**, **Never sooner than** N **minutes from now**, and at most how many **Times per reply**.
- **Appointment types it may offer**: **Any active type** or **Only the ones I choose**; click **Save types**.

## Trying it

In the test chat, pick a case under **Try** (**New enquiry**, **Came from an ad** or **Existing patient**), type in **Write as a patient…** and click **Send**. It uses what you've saved and the real free times in the calendar, and tells you if it looked up free times or would hand over to a person. Nothing is sent and nothing is booked. **Start over** clears the test conversation.

## In the Inbox

::help-video{clip="growth--receptionist-approve-draft" caption="Approving a receptionist draft in the Inbox"}
::

- **Draft ready**: the receptionist has written a reply. Open the conversation (or use the **Draft ready** filter) and click **Approve and send**, **Edit** or **Discard**. Nothing sends without your approval.
- **Draft a reply**: on a patient conversation, asks it for a draft when you need one.
- **AI handling**: it's running the conversation by itself. Click **Take over** to answer yourself; it won't reply again until you click **Hand back to AI**.
- **Needs human**: "The receptionist handed this to a person". Deal with it and click **I've handled it**, or **Hand back to AI**.

When it books, moves or hands over a conversation, it leaves an internal note in it so the team can see. Everything it has done also appears under **Overview › What it has done**.

> **Good to know:** if you reply in a conversation where it answered last, it stays quiet in that conversation for an hour.

> **Good to know:** the 24-hour rule of WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger still applies: outside it, it can't answer with free text.

## How many replies are included

**Replies this month** shows how many it has written out of those included (1,000 a month). Every reply it writes counts, sent or left as a draft. Replies beyond that are charged in blocks the following month; the price is on your subscription. To cap it, set a **Monthly limit** and click **Save**: once it's reached, it stops writing until the 1st.
