With two-factor login, a password is not enough to get into QuiroFlow: you also need a 6-digit code generated by an authenticator app on your phone. That way, if someone gets hold of your password, they can't open your account or see your patients' data. Each person turns it on for their own account, and an owner can require it for the whole team.

## Turning it on for your account

::help-video{clip="security--enable-2fa" caption="Turning on two-factor login with an authenticator app"}
::

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password or the iPhone's Passwords app all work.
2. In QuiroFlow, open the menu under your photo › **Account Settings** and go to **Security**.
3. Next to **Two-factor authentication** (**Off**), click **Set up two-factor**.
4. Scan the QR code with the app. If you can't scan it, click **Copy key** and paste it into the app (**Can't scan it? Enter this key:**). If you're doing this from your phone, **Add to authenticator app on this phone** does it for you.
5. Type the 6-digit code the app shows into **Code from the app**.
6. Click **Turn on two-factor**.

You'll see **Two-factor authentication is on.** and, under **Security**, the date you've been using it since. From now on, every time you sign in it asks for the code after your password. See [Signing in and resetting your password](/articles/getting-started/signing-in).

## Turning it off

Under **Security**, click **Turn off…** and confirm with **Turn off** (or **Keep it on** if you change your mind). Your password alone will be enough to sign in again. If your clinic requires it, you can't turn it off: you'll see **Your clinic requires two-factor authentication, so it stays on.**

## Requiring it for the whole team

::help-video{clip="security--require-2fa" caption="Requiring two-factor login for the whole team"}
::

You need the **Team** permission, and you must have two-factor turned on yourself and have signed in with it.

1. Go to **Settings → Team**. The **No two-factor** filter shows who doesn't have it yet.
2. At the bottom, turn on **Require two-factor authentication**.
3. The dialog tells you who will have to set it up. Click **Require it**.

The next time someone without two-factor opens QuiroFlow (on the computer or in the app), they'll have to set it up before carrying on. To make it optional again, turn the switch off: it saves straight away.

> **Good to know:** if the switch won't let you turn it on, it's because you don't have two-factor yet, or you haven't signed in with it in this session. Set it up first in your **Account Settings**, sign out and sign back in.

> **Good to know:** this isn't just a screen. Until someone enters the code, QuiroFlow shows them none of the clinic's data, not even to someone who tried to bypass the app using a stolen password.

## If someone loses their phone

Without the phone there's no code. Someone with the **Team** permission can reset it:

1. Go to **Settings → Team** and open the person's profile.
2. Under **Security**, next to **Two-factor: set up**, click **Reset two-factor** and confirm.

Their authenticator app stops working for QuiroFlow. If your clinic requires two-factor, they'll set it up again the next time they sign in; if not, they'll sign in with just their password until they turn it on again.

> **Good to know:** only an owner can reset another owner's two-factor. If the only owner loses their phone and nobody else can do it, write to **hola@quiroflow.com** from the account's email address and we'll help you get back in. Naming a second owner keeps you from ending up in that situation.

Every time someone turns two-factor on or off, or enters the code, it's recorded in the [activity log](/articles/security-privacy/activity-log).
