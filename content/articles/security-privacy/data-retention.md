A clinical record isn't kept forever: the law requires keeping it for a minimum number of years and, once that period has passed, the clinic should decide whether to keep it (with a reason) or anonymise it. The **Settings → Privacy** page helps you do this: it tells you which records are past their period and lets you keep or anonymise them. And if a patient asks for all of their data, you can download it from their record.

Only owners can do any of this.

## Choosing the retention period

::help-video{clip="security--retention" caption="Setting the retention period and keeping a record that is past its period"}
::

1. Go to **Settings → Privacy** (in the left menu, under **Data**).
2. Under **How long records are kept**, enter the **Years since the last visit, note, file or payment** (between 5 and 50).
3. Click **Save**.

> **Good to know:** national law (41/2002) requires at least 5 years from discharge, and some autonomous communities require more. Check yours.

## Reviewing records past their period

**Records past their period** lists the patients whose last activity (visit, note, file, document or payment) is older than your period, how many years ago it was, what their record contains and whether they have facturas.

Tick the ones you want (or **Select all**) and choose:

- **Keep**: you keep them longer. You choose **For** how many years and a **Reason** (for example, "foreseeable treatment"). They move to **Being kept**, where you can take them out with **Stop keeping**.
- **Anonymise**: their clinical notes, files, photos, documents, messages and contact details are deleted for good, and the name becomes "Paciente anonimizado". Their visits and their facturas, receipts and payments stay as they are, because the tax authority and VeriFactu require them separately.

> **Good to know:** anonymising can't be undone. The activity log still says who did what, but no longer what the record said.

## Consents that are about to expire

On the same page, **Consents about to expire** lists those that expire in the next 30 days, based on the validity you set on each template (**Settings → Documents**). Click **Renew** to go to that patient's documents and ask them to sign again. See [Documents and consents](/articles/patients/patient-documents).

## Handing a patient all of their data

If a patient exercises their right of access and asks for everything you hold about them:

1. Open their record.
2. In the **More actions** menu (the three dots), click **Export their data (GDPR)**.
3. QuiroFlow prepares a ZIP file with everything you have on that patient and downloads it.

The export is noted in the [activity log](/articles/security-privacy/activity-log). For the rest of the record, see [The patient record](/articles/patients/patient-records).

## Where your data lives

In **Settings → Privacy**, the **Security & data protection** link explains where the data is stored, how it's protected and which providers process it.
