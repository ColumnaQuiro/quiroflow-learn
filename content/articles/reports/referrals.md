**Referrals** lists the patients who arrived recommended by another patient: who brought whom, whether the referred person has already come to their first visit and whether you've already given the reward to whoever recommended them. You'll find it under **Reports › Growth & Performance**.

::help-video{clip="reports--referrals" caption="Seeing who refers people and marking a reward as given"}
::

## Where referrals come from

A patient appears here when their record says who referred them. That happens in two ways:

- They arrived with another patient's referral link, with the referral programme switched on in **Settings › Online Booking** (see [Setting up online booking](/articles/online-booking/setting-up-online-booking)). The **Programme settings** link, at the top right of the report, takes you there.
- Someone at the clinic set **Referred by** on the patient's record.

If there are none, the report says so: "No referrals yet. Switch the programme on in Settings › Online Booking, or set "Referred by" on a patient.".

## Reading the report and giving the reward

1. Open **Reports › Referrals**.
2. At the top you see three figures: **Referred** (all the patients who arrived by referral), **Came to a visit** (those who already have at least one completed visit) and **Reward to give** (those who have already come and whose reward you haven't yet marked as given).
3. Below, **Top referrers:** names the five patients who have brought the most people, and how many.
4. In the table, each row is a referred patient: **Referred by**, **Friend**, **Since** (when their record was created), **First visit** (or **Not yet**) and **Reward**.
5. When you give the reward to the person who referred them, click **Mark given** on their row. It shows as "Given on …". If you make a mistake, click **Undo**.

> **Good to know:** the **Mark given** button only appears once the referred person has come to their first visit. Until then the row says **After their first visit**.

> **Good to know:** this report has no period or practitioner filter: it shows all referrals, from newest to oldest. What reward to give is up to the clinic; QuiroFlow just keeps count.
