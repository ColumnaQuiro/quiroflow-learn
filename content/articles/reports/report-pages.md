A **report page** is a dashboard of your own: the figures you usually look at together, on one page, with a shared period and a comparison with the previous period or with last year. For example, a "Monthly management" page with income, visits, new patients and conversion, which you look at on the 1st of every month. They sit at the very top of **Reports**, under **Report pages**.

## Creating a page

::help-video{clip="reports--report-page-create" caption="Creating a page from the Monthly management template"}
::

1. In **Reports**, click **+ New report page** (top right) or **+ New page**. You can also click the name of a template directly in the line "— start empty, or from a template:".
2. In **New report page**, type the **Name**.
3. Under **Start from**, choose **An empty page** or a template:
   - **Monthly management**: income, visits, new patients and conversion.
   - **Practitioner performance**: visits, income and PVA per practitioner.
   - **Growth & leads**: leads, the funnel and where new patients come from.
4. Under **Who can see it**, choose **Everyone in the clinic with access to reports** or **Only me**.
5. Click **Create**. A page made from a template opens already with its blocks; an empty one opens in edit mode.

## Using a page

- **The period**: **This month**, **Last month**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 3 months** or **Last 12 months**. It changes all the blocks at once.
- **Compare with**: **No comparison**, **Compare: previous period** or **Compare: a year before**. Each figure then shows how much it has changed ("vs previous period", "vs a year before").
- **Download PDF**: the whole page as a PDF.
- **More**: **Duplicate** the page, **Share with the whole clinic** or **Make it only mine**, and **Delete page**.

Each block counts the same as the standard report it comes from: a block's income is the one in [Income and payments](/articles/reports/income-and-payments), conversions are the ones in [Statistics](/articles/reports/statistics), and so on for all of them.

## Adding and arranging blocks

::help-video{clip="reports--report-page-block" caption="Adding a new block: income per practitioner as bars"}
::

1. On the page, click **Edit page**. At the top it will say **Editing**.
2. Click **Add a block here** where you want it.
3. In **Add a block**, search ("Search: income, retention, no-show…") and choose one under **From reports** (the sections of the standard reports) or **Saved** (the ones the clinic has saved), and click **Add**.
4. For a custom one, click **New report**:
   - **What to measure**: choose the figure (you can search all the metrics: income collected, first visits, attendance rate, bono debt, new leads…).
   - **Split by**: by month, by practitioner, by payment method, by appointment type… or none.
   - **Show as**: **Number**, **Bar**, **Line**, **Donut**, **Table** or **Funnel**.
   - **Period**: the page's, or **Its own:** one of its own for this block (for example **Last 6 months** or **This year**).
   - **Only include**: a **Practitioner**, a **Clinic** or a **Payment method**.
   - **Title**, and check the **Preview**.
   - Tick **Also save to Saved reports, to use on other pages** if you want to reuse it.
   - Click **Add to page**.
5. Drag a block to move it. In its menu (**Block options**) you have **Edit…**, **Duplicate**, **Move earlier**, **Move later**, **Width** and **Remove**.
6. Click **Done**. Changes save themselves: at the top you'll see **Saved**.

> **Good to know:** only whoever created the page, or an owner, can edit it, change who sees it or delete it. Everyone else can see it and download it.

> **Good to know:** each block respects your role. If you have **Only their own figures**, the blocks count only your figures, and those that are about the whole clinic say "Not available with your role: this figure is about the whole clinic.". A WhatsApp block requires **Inbox**, and lead and ad blocks say "Part of Growth, which this clinic does not have." if the clinic doesn't have Growth.

> **Good to know:** removing a block or deleting a page doesn't delete any data: "The figures themselves are not affected.".
