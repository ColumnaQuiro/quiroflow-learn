**Reports** groups everything into a few areas:

![Reports index page with cards grouped into Operations, Growth & Performance, Financial, and Data](/screenshots/reports-index.png)

## Scheduling

- **Scheduled Reminders** -- WhatsApp delivery status: who's confirmed, who's still pending, and who wants to reschedule. See its own article: [The Scheduled Reminders report](/articles/reports/the-scheduled-reminders-report).
- **Upcoming Visits** -- how booked appointments are distributed across the month.
- **Appointment Distribution** -- which shift or time of day performs best, by volume and completion rate.

## Performance

- **Statistics** -- first visits, reports, revisions, PVA, conversion, and retention.
- **Income Performance** -- compare practitioners and months side by side, with growth curves.

## Financial

- **Income & Payments** -- revenue broken down by day, week, month, or year, and by payment method, practitioner, or visit type.
- **Debtors** -- package (bono) purchases that have no fully paid invoice, and how much is outstanding.
- **Memberships** -- active membership count, monthly recurring revenue, and any failed payments.

## Data quality

- **Data Exports** -- patients missing an email, phone number, or a required consent/data-protection form.
- **Custom Reports** -- build and save your own: pick a data source, a metric, and a chart type.
