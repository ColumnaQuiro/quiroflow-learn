**Reports** brings together the clinic's figures: the money coming in, how the diary fills up, who comes back and which messages go out. Each report has its own filters and its own date range, so you can look at last month in one and the last week in another without them interfering. Open it from **Reports** in the sidebar.

::

## What is on the page

At the top are the **Report pages**: your own dashboards, with the figures you usually look at together. They are explained in [Report pages](/articles/reports/report-pages).

Below, under **Standard reports**, are the ready-made reports, in four groups.

**Operations**
- **Scheduled Reminders**: whether the WhatsApp messages went out and who has confirmed their appointment. See [Scheduled Reminders](/articles/reports/the-scheduled-reminders-report).
- **Communications**: every message sent to patients and leads. See [Communications](/articles/reports/communications-report).
- **Upcoming Visits**, **Patient Flow** and **Appointment Distribution**: how the diary is spread, how long patients wait and which times of day perform best. See [Diary reports](/articles/reports/appointment-reports).

**Growth & Performance**
- **Retention** and **Recalls**: who comes back, who leaves their plan unfinished and what the recall automations bring back. See [Retention and Recalls](/articles/reports/retention-and-recalls).
- **Referrals**: who brought whom. See [Referrals](/articles/reports/referrals).
- **Statistics**: first visits, reports, revisions, PVA, conversion and retention. See [Statistics](/articles/reports/statistics).
- **Income Performance**: each practitioner's income month by month. See [Income & Payments](/articles/reports/income-and-payments).

**Financial**
- **Income & Payments**: what was charged, what was paid and what is outstanding. See [Income & Payments](/articles/reports/income-and-payments).
- **Commissions**: what each person earned from their pay plan. See [Commissions](/articles/reports/commissions).
- **Daily Transactions**: a day's payments, for cashing up. See [Daily Transactions](/articles/reports/daily-transactions).
- **Debtors** and **Memberships**: what is owed on bonos and how memberships are doing. See [Debtors and Memberships](/articles/reports/debtors-and-memberships).
- **Third parties owed**: what insurers, mutuas and companies owe. It takes you to Billing › Third parties; see [Billing & payments](/articles/billing-payments).

**Data**
- **Issued facturas** and **Accountant**: the facturas book and the quarter's IVA for your accountant (gestoría). See [Issued facturas and Accountant](/articles/reports/facturas-and-gestoria).
- **Data Exports**: get the patient list as a CSV, or find incomplete records. See [Data Exports](/articles/reports/data-exports).

## Changing the date range and filters

Most reports share the same bar at the top:

- **The date range**: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **This month** (selected by default on almost all of them) or **Last month**, or your own dates under **Custom range**.
- **All practitioners** and **All clinics**: to see only one person's or one site's figures.
- **Download PDF**: saves the report as you see it, with the date range and filters written at the top. While it is being made the button says **Preparing…**.

::help-video{clip="reports--period-and-filters" caption="Changing the date range and filtering by practitioner"}
::

1. Click the date range button (the one with the calendar icon).
2. Pick an option, or type both dates under **Custom range** and click **Apply**.
3. If you like, pick a practitioner or a clinic in the drop-downs beside it. The figures recalculate straight away.
4. Click **Download PDF** to save or send it.

> **Good to know:** "This month" runs from the 1st to today, not to the end of the month. To compare whole months, use **Last month** or a custom range.

## Who can see each report

- To open Reports you need the **See reports** permission in your role (see [Roles and permissions](/articles/team-roles/roles-and-permissions)).
- If your role has **Only their own figures**, the page tells you: "Your role shows you only your own figures: your appointments, your takings and your patients." The practitioner filter is fixed to **Only your own figures**, and the reports that only make sense for the whole clinic are not shown: Communications, Scheduled Reminders, Debtors, Memberships, Recalls, Issued facturas and Data Exports.
- **Commissions** is for owners only.
- **Scheduled Reminders** also needs the **Inbox** permission; **Accountant** and **Third parties owed** also need **Charge and issue receipts**.

> **Good to know:** the figures come from what is recorded in the diary and at the till. If an appointment stays booked even though the patient came, or a payment isn't recorded, the report cannot know. Marking appointments and payments properly every day is what makes the reports add up.
