**Reports** groups everything into a few areas, from day-to-day scheduling questions to financial totals to data-quality checks. Each report has its own filters and date range, so you're not stuck with one fixed window across all of them.

![Reports index page with cards grouped into Operations, Growth & Performance, Financial, and Data](/screenshots/reports-index.png)

## Operations

- **Scheduled Reminders** -- WhatsApp delivery status: who's confirmed, who's still pending, and who wants to reschedule. See its own article: [The Scheduled Reminders report](/articles/reports/the-scheduled-reminders-report).
- **Upcoming Visits** -- how booked appointments are distributed across the month, useful for spotting a week that's unusually light or overbooked before it happens.
- **Appointment Distribution** -- which shift or time of day performs best, by volume and completion rate. Handy when deciding whether to open more slots in a morning or afternoon block.

## Growth & Performance

- **Statistics** -- first visits, reports, revisions, PVA (average visits per patient), conversion, and retention. This is the closest thing to a single "how's the practice doing" dashboard beyond the main Dashboard page itself.
- **Income Performance** -- compare practitioners and months side by side, with growth curves. Good for spotting which practitioner's book is growing versus flat, without pulling numbers manually from each one's calendar.

## Financial

- **Income & Payments** -- revenue broken down by day, week, month, or year, and by payment method, practitioner, or visit type. This is usually the report to reach for when reconciling what actually came in against what your bank shows.
- **Debtors** -- package (bono) purchases that have no fully paid invoice, and how much is outstanding. Since QuiroFlow doesn't automatically chase a partially-paid package (see [Selling a package to a patient](/articles/billing-payments/selling-a-package-bono)), this report is the practical way to see who still owes money without checking every patient's Billing tab individually.
- **Memberships** -- active membership count, monthly recurring revenue, and any failed payments. Check this periodically even if you're not actively managing memberships day to day -- a failed payment here often means a patient's card expired and needs updating.

## Data quality

- **Data Exports** -- patients missing an email, phone number, or a required consent/data-protection form. Worth a periodic check, since a patient missing a phone number can't receive WhatsApp confirmations or reminders at all.
- **Custom Reports** -- build and save your own: pick a data source, a metric, and a chart type. Use this when none of the built-in reports quite answer the specific question you have.

## Where to start

If you're only going to check one report regularly, **Income & Payments** and **Scheduled Reminders** cover the two things that most affect day-to-day operations: money coming in, and whether tomorrow's patients actually know they're booked.
