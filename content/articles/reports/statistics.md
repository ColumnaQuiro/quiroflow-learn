**Statistics** counts the visits in each stage of treatment (first visits, reports, adjustments, revisions, maintenance) and works out how patients move from one to the next. It's the snapshot of how the practice is doing. You'll find it under **Reports › Growth & Performance**.

::

## Before you start: each appointment type's stage

Statistics doesn't look at the appointment's name, but at the **Treatment stage** of its appointment type: **First visit**, **First visit (offer)**, **Report / exam findings**, **Revision / check-up**, **Maintenance**, **Adjustment** or **Other**. It's set in **Settings › Appointment Types**, when you open each type (see [Appointment types and services](/articles/settings/appointment-types-and-services)).

If any type has no stage, the report warns you at the top in yellow: "Not counted anywhere below, because they have no stage yet: …", with a link to **Settings → Appointment Types**.

## Reading the report

1. Open **Reports › Statistics**.
2. Choose the period and, if you like, a practitioner or a clinic.
3. Read the cards. Each one compares with the same dates of the previous month (the sentence at the top says which exact dates): a green ▲ or red ▼ arrow with the percentage and "vs N", the figure back then.
4. Scroll down to **Month by month** and hover over the chart to see each month.

## What each figure counts

Everything counts **attended visits**: appointments marked as completed. A booked appointment the patient hasn't yet attended doesn't count.

- **First visits**, **First visit offers**, **Reports**, **Adjustments** and **Maintenance visits**: the period's completed visits with that stage. Underneath, "+N booked" are those still on the schedule in those dates without being completed, and "N cancelled" includes cancelled and no-shows. That's how you understand why the figure is lower than what you see on the calendar.
- **Revision 1** and **Revision 2**: each patient's first and second revision over their whole history that fall within the period. It works the same whether you have a single "Revision" type or two separate types.
- **Total completed visits**: all the period's completed visits, including those of types with no stage.
- **PVA (visits / new patient)**: the period's completed visits divided by the new patients (those who had a first visit or a first visit with offer in the period). Underneath it gives the two figures: "N visits · N new patients".
- **Conversion to 3rd visit**: of the patients who received their report in the period, how many later came to an adjustment, even if the adjustment was already in the following month.
- **Retention post-revision**: of the patients who had their revision 1 in the period, how many later moved on to a maintenance visit.
- **Overall retention**: of the patients seen in the period, what percentage had already come before. In other words, how many are returning patients rather than new ones.

The **Month by month** chart draws the three rates and the PVA for the last 12 months, one point per month. It doesn't depend on the period chosen at the top.

> **Good to know:** the practitioner filter counts only that person's visits, for conversion and retention too. If one practitioner did the report and another the adjustment, the conversion doesn't appear for either of them separately.

> **Good to know:** Statistics doesn't count money or bono sales. For that, see [Income and payments](/articles/reports/income-and-payments) and [Debtors and Memberships](/articles/reports/debtors-and-memberships).
