Visit notes are the patient's clinical history: one note per appointment, with the date, the visit type and who saw them. They're always written against an appointment, and the place built for it is **My Day**, the practitioner's screen, which puts everything you need in front of you while you treat: the previous visit, today's pain, the care plan and the flags.

## Write the note in My Day

::help-video{clip="clinical--visit-note-myday" caption="Writing the visit note from My Day"}
::

1. Open **My Day** and click the patient's appointment.
2. Under **Today**, set **Pain today** from 0 to 10. If the patient already gave it when checking in, you'll see "The patient gave N at check-in."
3. Write the note in its four sections: **Subjective**, **Objective**, **Action** and **Plan**. To help you:
   - **Quick add**: common phrases inserted with one click.
   - **Datestamp**, **New Line**, **Undo Last** and **Clear All**.
   - **Listings**: tap the segments adjusted; they write themselves into **Action**. **Same as last time** repeats the previous visit's, and **+ Extremities** adds the extremities.
4. **Last visit** shows last time's note and listing. Click **Bring over** on a section to copy it into today's note, or **Use today** on the listing to repeat it. **Copy last note**, at the top, copies the whole previous note.
5. If the patient has filled in forms, under **Forms** you can **Bring** their answers into **Subjective**.
6. Click **Save note** to save without closing the visit, or **Sign & complete** to save the note and mark the appointment completed.

Alongside you have the patient's attendance, their care plan, their **Flags**, a **Quick Note**, the **Notes History** and **Files & Documents**. **View profile →** opens their record.

> **Good to know:** when the care plan has two visits or fewer left, **My Day** shows what comes next at the top (maintenance, same again or discharge). See [Care plans](/articles/clinical/care-plans).

For everything else in **My Day** (the day's list, alerts, tasks), see [My Day](/articles/calendar-appointments/my-day).

## Other ways to write a note

- **From the calendar**: open the appointment and go to its notes. See [Booking an appointment](/articles/calendar-appointments/booking-an-appointment).
- **From the record**: on **Clinical**, click **Add note**, type in **Add a note…** and click **Add note**. It's a free-text note, without sections.

> **Good to know:** **Add note** on the record saves the note to the patient's most recent appointment by date, and that can be a future appointment they already have booked. To note a specific visit, do it from that appointment in **My Day** or the calendar.

## Read the history

::help-video{clip="clinical--visit-notes-history" caption="Reading notes from earlier visits"}
::

On the record's **Clinical** tab, **Visit notes** lists every note, newest first, with the date and visit type. The newest opens by itself; click any other to see it with its sections (**Subjective**, **Objective**, **Action**, **Plan**), who wrote it and when.

If a patient has no notes yet, you'll see "No visit notes yet — these are written against an appointment."

> **Good to know:** notes written in **My Day** show split into sections. Free-text notes (or notes imported from another system) show exactly as they were written.

## Editing and deleting notes

Treat notes as what they are, a clinical record: correct them if there's a mistake, but don't use them as a draft. What each person can do is set by their role in **Settings › Roles & Permissions**:

- **See visit notes**: without it, notes don't appear on the record or on the appointment.
- **Write and edit notes**.
- **Delete notes**.
- **Which notes they can edit or delete**: **Anyone's** or **Only their own**.

See [Roles and permissions](/articles/team-roles/roles-and-permissions).
