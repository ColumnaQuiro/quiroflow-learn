The record's **Clinical** tab brings the patient's clinical information together in one place: the clinical summary (why they came, what's wrong, what they want to achieve and what to watch for), the notes from each visit, their care plan, the permanent note, the readings you've sent them and their home exercises.

::

## The clinical summary

At the top you see **Chief complaint**, **Working diagnosis**, **Red flag** and **Yellow flag**. To change them:

1. Click **Edit clinical details**.
2. Fill in what applies:
   - **Chief complaint**: why they came.
   - **Working diagnosis**.
   - **Treatment goals**: what they want to achieve ("Run 10 km again", "Sleep without pain").
   - **Red flags**: urgent findings requiring immediate attention.
   - **Yellow flags**: psychosocial or risk factors to keep in mind.
   - **Tags**, with **+ Add tag**.
3. Each field saves when you leave it. Click **Done** to close.

Red and yellow flags also show on the **Overview** tab and in **My Day**, so whoever sees the patient has them in front of them. Goals appear as chips in the right-hand column.

The chief complaint and diagnosis are also used to suggest readings (see [Patient education](/articles/clinical/education)) and can be inserted into letters.

## Your clinic's own fields

If your clinic has created its own clinical fields in **Settings › Custom Fields** (for example "Sport they play" or "Handedness"), they appear on the **Clinic fields** card. Click **Edit**, fill them in and **Save**. Only people who can see clinical notes see them.

## The patient note

The **Patient note**, in the right-hand column, is a permanent note for whatever is always worth knowing about this patient: "Prefers afternoon appointments", "Bringing MRI report", "Sensitive to cervical manipulation". Click **Edit**, type, and click **Done**.

It also shows in the calendar when you hover over any of this patient's appointments.

### QuiroFlow's three notes

They're easy to mix up:

- **Visit notes**: clinical, one per appointment, and part of the patient's history. See [Visit notes](/articles/clinical/visit-notes).
- **Patient note**: just one, permanent, for context that always matters. Editing it replaces what was there.
- **Appointment note**: a working note on one appointment in the calendar (for example "bringing her son"), which stops mattering once the appointment is over.

A simple rule: if it will still matter a year from now as part of their treatment, it's a visit note. If it's always relevant, it's the patient note. If it only matters for that appointment, it's the appointment note.

## Who can see and edit it

- Editing the clinical summary and the patient note needs the **Edit patient details** permission.
- Visit notes, the **Progress** tab and the clinic's own clinical fields are only visible to people with the **See visit notes** permission.
