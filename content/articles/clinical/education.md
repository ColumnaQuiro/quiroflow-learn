Readings are short texts and videos that explain to patients what's going on and how to look after themselves: what to expect at the first visit, what to do about low back pain, how to sit at work. You prepare them once in the clinic's library and send them to each patient from their record. They reach their app; if they don't have it, by email or as a link.

## Set up the library

Go to **Settings › Education** (you need the **Clinic and diary** permission).

::help-video{clip="clinical--education-library" caption="Adding QuiroFlow's readings to your library"}
::

**Start with QuiroFlow's.** If you don't have them yet, you'll see an offer to start with QuiroFlow's readings (first visit, low back, neck, headache, posture at work, pregnancy…). Click **Add the N** and they land in your library for you to edit as you like.

**Write your own.** Click **New article** and fill in:

1. **Title** and one line on what it's about (optional).
2. **Text**: a blank line starts a new paragraph.
3. **Video link, YouTube or Vimeo (optional)**: only those two play inside the reading; any other link is shown as a link.
4. **Reasons for visit it is for**: which cases it's for (used to suggest it on the record).
5. **Minutes** of reading (1 to 60).
6. **Exercise programme to go with it (optional)**.
7. Click **Save**.

The **In the library** list can be filtered by reason (**Filter by reason for visit**) and shows how many times each reading has been sent. From there you can **Edit**, **Archive** or **Delete**; archived ones can be brought back with **Restore**.

## Send a reading to a patient

::help-video{clip="clinical--education-send" caption="Sending a reading from the record"}
::

1. On the record, open **Clinical** and, under **Reading**, click **+ Send**.
2. Choose the **Article**. The ones that match their chief complaint or diagnosis are starred (**★ Suggested for their reason for visit.**).
3. Click **Send**. Depending on what the patient has, you'll see:
   - **Sent to their app.**
   - **They have no app: sent by email.**
   - **No app and no email: copy the link and send it by WhatsApp.**, with a **Copy link** button.

The record's **Reading** list shows each one sent as **Read** or **Not opened**.

## What the patient sees

In their app and portal, the patient sees a **For you** card with new readings (**New**). If the reading comes with an exercise programme, they also see **Your exercises**.

If they get it by email or link, the reading opens on a page with no need to sign in. If it comes with exercises, the page reminds them to ask for them at their next visit or in the clinic's app.
