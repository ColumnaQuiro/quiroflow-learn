The posture screen turns a few photos of the patient standing into measurements: how far forward the head sits, how much it effectively weighs on the neck, shoulder and pelvic tilt, knee alignment. It's for documenting the starting point, showing the patient what you see, and checking how they improve assessment by assessment.

> **Good to know:** these are estimates from photos, to document and explain, not to diagnose.

## Take an assessment

1. On the record, open **Progress** and, in the **Posture** section, click **New assessment**.
2. Enter the patient's **Height**. That's what lets the photo's measurements be turned into centimetres.
3. Take the photos in this order: **Frente** (front), **Lado derecho** (right side), **Espalda** (back) and, if you like, **Lado izquierdo** (left side). For each, click **Take photo** or **Choose from gallery**.
   - Standing, relaxed, arms by the sides, feet hip-width apart, barefoot if possible.
   - Whole body in the picture, head to feet, against a plain wall.
   - Camera upright at hip height, 2–3 m away, the same place every time.
4. QuiroFlow finds the points by itself (**Finding the points…**): ears, shoulders, hips, knees and ankles. If one is off, drag it into place. The blue line is the vertical from the ankle.
5. The measurements update live: **Effective head weight**, **Ear–shoulder angle**, shoulder and pelvic tilt, offsets and **Knees**.
6. Click **Next** for the next photo, **Retake** if you want another, or **Skip to the result**. You need at least the front photo and one side.
7. Add **Notes (optional)** and click **Save assessment**.

On a phone (with the staff app) you can take the photos straight from the camera.

## View and compare assessments

::help-video{clip="clinical--posture" caption="Comparing two posture assessments and sharing them with the patient"}
::

**Progress** › **Posture** lists all the patient's assessments. For each one:

- **Compare** with another date, to see the photos and measurements side by side.
- **Patient can see it**: tick it so they see it in their app and portal, under **Your progress**.
- **Report (PDF)**: a report with the photos and measurements to hand them.
- **Delete**: removes the assessment and its photos.

With two or more assessments, **Progress** shows a chart of how one measurement changes at each assessment, with a summary such as "Closer to aligned: 60% of the way." The dashed green line is aligned: the closer to it, the better.

## X-rays

If you've uploaded X-rays to **Attachments** › **Files** and ticked **X-ray** on them, they appear under **Posture** › **X-rays**. Pick two to see them side by side. See [Patient files and photos](/articles/patients/patient-files).

## What the patient sees

Assessments you tick **Patient can see it** appear in their app and portal under **Your progress**: their photos, their posture report as a PDF, and whether each measurement is better, the same, or one to watch. See [The patient portal](/articles/patient-app/patient-portal).

You need the **See visit notes** permission.
