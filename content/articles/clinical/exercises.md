Home exercises are set up once in the clinic's library and assigned to each patient from their record, with the sets, reps and frequency that suit them. The patient sees them in their app and portal with a video or photo, and ticks them off the days they do them. That way you can see whether they're keeping up, and QuiroFlow tells you if they stop.

## Build the library

Go to **Settings › Exercise Library** (you need the **Clinic and diary** permission).

::help-video{clip="clinical--exercise-library" caption="Adding exercises from the QuiroFlow catalogue to your library"}
::

**From the QuiroFlow catalogue.** Under **QuiroFlow catalogue** you'll find ready-made exercises with a demonstration video, and complete programmes. Click **Add** on an exercise or **Add programme** on a programme: it's copied into your library (you'll see **✓ In library**) and from there you can edit it like any other. Patients see the demonstration video unless you upload your own.

**Your own exercises.** Click **New exercise** and fill in:

1. **Name**.
2. **How to do it**: the instructions for the patient.
3. A video or photo: paste a **Video or image link (optional)** (it has to start with https://) or click **+ Upload a video or photo** (up to 50 MB).
4. Click **Save**.

**In the library** shows each exercise with its kind of video or photo and how many patients are doing it right now (**Doing it now**). A change to an exercise reaches every patient who has it, in the app and in the portal.

**Programmes.** A programme is a set of exercises with their dose, assigned in one go (for example "Low back basics"). Click **New programme**, give it a **Name** and, if you like, **For (optional, only staff see it)**; add the exercises with **+ Add exercise**, each with **Sets**, **Reps or time**, **How often** or **Progressive**, and click **Save**. Editing a programme doesn't change what patients who were given it already have.

> **Good to know:** an exercise any patient has ever had can only be archived with **Archive** (so their history keeps its ticked days). **Delete** only appears on archived exercises that were never assigned and aren't in any programme.

## Assign exercises to a patient

::help-video{clip="clinical--exercise-assign" caption="Assigning an exercise programme to a patient"}
::

1. On the record, open **Clinical** and scroll down to **Home exercises**.
2. Click **Assign exercises**.
3. Search in **Search exercises or programmes**. You'll see **Your programmes**, **QuiroFlow programmes** and **Your library**. Picking a programme ticks all its exercises; you can also tick single exercises (**Already has it** marks the ones they already have). If one is missing, create it right there with **+ New exercise**.
4. Check each one's dose under **Chosen**: **Sets**, **Reps or time** (or **No fixed number**), **How often**, **Progressive: they build up as they can** and a **Note for the patient (optional)**.
5. Click **Assign N**.

Each assigned exercise shows the **Last seven days** ("3 of 7 days") from what the patient ticks, and a **Remove** button to take it off them. **Save these as a programme…** saves this patient's selection as a new programme to reuse.

## What the patient sees

In the app (on **Home** › **Your exercises**) and in the portal (**Exercises**), the patient sees "What your clinic asked you to do at home. Tick each one off the days you do it." Each exercise has **How to do it**, **▶ Watch the video** and a **Done today** button, and they see their last seven days. In the app they can switch on a **Daily reminder** for days they haven't ticked them yet.

> **Good to know:** patients with the app who haven't ticked any exercise for 5 days or more appear under **Patients › Care Plan Alerts** › **Home exercises not being done**. See [Care plans](/articles/clinical/care-plans).
