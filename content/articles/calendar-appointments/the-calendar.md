The **Calendar** is the clinic's diary. The front desk and practitioners book, move, check in, charge and cancel visits from here, and see at a glance who is waiting, who hasn't confirmed and who owes money. You'll find it in the sidebar under **Today** › **Calendar**.

## What's on screen

- **Top bar:** the ‹ › arrows and **Today** to move through dates, the view picker, and the buttons **Cash shift**, **Block time**, **Unplanned absence**, **New class** and **+ New Appointment**.
- **Practitioner tabs:** **Whole clinic**, one tab per practitioner, and **No practitioner** (visits with nobody assigned).
- **Counts row:** above the grid, how many visits are in what you're looking at ("48 appointments", or "12 appointments today") and a set of stage buttons: **unconfirmed**, **want to move**, **waiting**, **in session**, **to pay** and **owe**. Each one only shows when at least one visit is in that stage.
- **The grid:** one column per room (plus **Unassigned**) for each day, from 08:00 to 20:00. A red line marks the current time. Under each room's name you can see who is working in it that day.
- **Left panel:** a mini month (click a day to jump to it), the [Flow tracker](/articles/calendar-appointments/flow-tracker), **Today at a glance**, the **Display** switches and the **Stage key**. On narrower screens this panel opens from the **Info** button.

Hours nobody works are hatched and labelled **Nobody working**, and blocked time is grey (**Blocked**, **Blocked (whole clinic)** or **Blocked for** followed by a name).

## Switch view and practitioner

::help-video{clip="calendar--views-and-tabs" caption="Going from the week to a day, to one practitioner's diary and to the month"}
::

1. Open the view picker (it reads **Work week** the first time) and choose **Day**, **Work week** (Monday to Friday), **Week** (Monday to Sunday) or **Month**.
2. Click a practitioner's tab to see only their diary, or **Whole clinic** to see everyone's.
3. In **Month** view, each day shows how many visits it has, how full it is and the free hours left. A day with no time left says **Full**, and a day the clinic is shut says **Closed**. Click a day to open it in Day view.

The view and tab you choose are remembered in that browser the next time you open the calendar.

> **Good to know:** month occupancy is worked out from the practitioners' hours. If nobody has hours set, you'll see "Set working hours in Settings › Team to see how full each day is." See [Practitioner working hours](/articles/team-roles/practitioner-working-hours).

In **Day** view up to six overlapping visits sit side by side, and up to four in the week views. Beyond that they collapse into a "+N more" button that opens that time in Day view. In week view, each day's header also shows how many visits are **unconfirmed** and how many patients **owe** money.

## Filter by stage with the counts row

::help-video{clip="calendar--count-filter" caption="Highlighting the unconfirmed visits, then showing them all again"}
::

1. Click one of the buttons in the counts row, for example **unconfirmed**.
2. Visits that don't match are dimmed (they don't disappear), so the ones you're after stand out.
3. Click **Show all** to clear the filter.

Only one filter works at a time. **unconfirmed** also counts online bookings that haven't been confirmed yet, and **owe** shows the total those patients owe.

## Reading an appointment block

Each visit is a block with the patient's name (shortened when it doesn't fit, like "M. Gil") and the time. Before you even open it, it tells you a lot:

- **The border** shows whether the visit is firm: dashed while it is unconfirmed, booked online or the patient wants to move it; solid once it is confirmed.
- **The pill** says where the patient is: **Unconfirmed**, **Confirmed**, **Arrived** with the time, **In session**, **To pay**, **Done** or **No-show**.
- **Red means money, and nothing else:** an "Owes" badge with the amount.
- **The colour** is the appointment type's. Done visits keep their colour, greyed out.
- **Small icons:** red flags on the record, forms not filled in, a clinical note, **Moved before** (the visit has been moved at least once) and the care-plan marker ("Visit N" of the plan). A dashed **No next visit** chip warns that the patient has nothing else booked while they are in session, at checkout or just seen.

Every stage is explained in [Appointment stages](/articles/calendar-appointments/appointment-statuses-explained).

## See the details without opening the visit

::help-video{clip="calendar--hover-card" caption="Hovering over a visit to see its quick card"}
::

Hover over a block (or reach it with the keyboard) and a card appears with:

- name, time, practitioner and room;
- where it stands ("Unconfirmed · Reminder sent today 10:00 · no reply", for example);
- pending forms, the appointment type's **For the team** note and any **Red flags**;
- whether it has been moved ("Moved 2 times" and from when);
- money: what they owe, credit available or **Nothing owed**, and what this visit will cost;
- the bono this visit will use and the sessions left on it;
- the **Patient note**.

The card is read-only. Click (or press **Enter**) to open the visit. On a touch screen there's no card: a tap opens the visit straight away.

## Today at a glance

In the left panel, **Today at a glance** sums up today for the whole clinic: **Collected** and **Invoiced** (only for people allowed to see the day sheet in Reports; clicking opens it), and the visits **Booked**, **Seen**, **Rescheduled**, **Cancelled** and **Missed**, each with its percentage. It always describes today, even while you're looking at another day.

## Display switches

::help-video{clip="calendar--privacy-mode" caption="Turning on privacy mode to blur names"}
::

Under **Display** in the left panel:

| Switch | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Privacy mode** | Blurs patient names on the calendar and in the flow tracker. Useful when the waiting room can see the screen. | Off |
| **Flow tracker** | Shows or hides the [flow tracker](/articles/calendar-appointments/flow-tracker). | On |
| **Show availability** | Shows the grey bands of blocked time. **Nobody working** hours stay hatched. | On |
| **Show cancelled** | Brings back cancelled visits, which normally leave the grid so the slot is free. | Off |
| **Hide rescheduled** | Hides the "Moved to …" markers left at a moved visit's old time. | Off |
| **Hide deleted** | Hides deleted visits. | On |

> **Good to know:** these switches aren't saved. A page reload puts them back to their defaults, so if you use **Privacy mode** at the front desk, turn it on each time you open the calendar.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press `?` anywhere to see the full list (**Keyboard shortcuts**). On the calendar:

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `c` or `n` | New appointment |
| `t` | Go to today |
| `j` / `k` | Next / previous day or week |
| `d` / `w` / `m` | Day / work week / month view |
| arrows and **Enter** | Move between slots in the grid; **Enter** books a free slot or opens a visit |
| `a` (with a visit open) | Next step: check in, into session, to checkout |
| `p` (with a visit open) | Take payment |

From any page, `g` then `c` takes you to the calendar. Shortcuts do nothing while you are typing in a field.

## On a phone

On a phone-sized screen the calendar becomes a one-column agenda: at the top you choose **My appointments** or **Whole clinic**, finished visits fold into a single row, and a round **+** button creates a new visit. The **More** menu has the view, **New appointment**, **Block time**, **Cash shift** and **Today's info**.

## Who can do what

- The **Calendar** permission decides which visits each person sees: **All appointments**, **Only their own** or **Nothing** (then they see neither the Calendar nor My Day).
- With **Read-only calendar**, the person sees visits but can't book, move, edit or block: a **Read-only** pill replaces the buttons. They can still take payment.
- **Cash shift** only shows for people with **Record payments and run the till**. How it works is in [Cash shift](/articles/billing-payments/cash-shift).

Permissions are changed in [Roles and permissions](/articles/team-roles/roles-and-permissions).

> **Good to know:** the grid always runs from 08:00 to 20:00. A visit that starts before 08:00 or ends after 20:00 is drawn pinned to the edge.

> **Good to know:** on a practitioner's tab, hatched hours follow their own working hours (or the clinic's if they have none). On **Whole clinic**, an hour is only hatched when nobody works it.
