The **Dashboard** is the first thing you see when you open QuiroFlow: a greeting ("Good morning" and your name) and a board of widgets with the day-to-day figures, such as the week's visits, the month's income, who owes money or which patients are due a call. It's in the sidebar under **Today** › **Dashboard**, and each person arranges their own.

## What it shows

At the top there's a date-range picker (the last month by default) and a practitioner filter. Widgets tied to the week say "This week"; the rest follow the dates you choose.

The widgets you start with are:

1. **Visits this week**
2. **Income this month**
3. **Visit summary**
4. **Debtors**
5. **Next up today**
6. **Active patients**
7. **No-show rate**
8. **Recalls due**

You can also add **Care plans behind schedule**, **Total patients**, **Appointment distribution**, **Visit value & retention**, **Memberships**, **Appointment volume trend** and, for owners only, **Monthly goals**.

## Customise your dashboard

::help-video{clip="calendar--dashboard-layout" caption="Adding a widget to the dashboard, changing its width and saving the layout"}
::

1. Click **Edit layout**, top right.
2. Click **Add a widget** and pick one from the list. If you already have them all, you'll see "All widgets are already on your dashboard."
3. Drag a widget by its handle (**Drag to reorder**) to move it.
4. Use **Cycle widget width** to make it wider or narrower, or **Remove widget** to take it off.
5. Click **Done**.

The layout is saved for you: each person on the team has their own.

## A new account's first steps

On a brand-new account, the owner sees a card "Your first visit, three steps away":

1. **Add what you offer**, with **Add a type**.
2. **Add your first patient**, with **New patient** (or "Coming from PracticeHub? Import").
3. **Book their first visit**, with **Open the calendar**.

Each step ticks itself off once it's done, whether from here or from another screen. When you've finished, the card suggests turning on **Online booking**. **Hide** removes it. First steps are covered in [First steps](/articles/getting-started/first-steps).

## What each person sees

The **Dashboard** permission decides which figures appear:

- **Everything**: the whole clinic's figures.
- **Only their own**: every widget shows only their visits, takings and patients; the practitioner filter is locked to them, and the bono-debt and membership widgets are left out.
- **Nothing**: the dashboard says "No figures on your dashboard", and everything else their role allows stays in the menu.

See [Roles and permissions](/articles/team-roles/roles-and-permissions).

> **Good to know:** if someone stops being an owner, the **Monthly goals** widget disappears from their dashboard.
