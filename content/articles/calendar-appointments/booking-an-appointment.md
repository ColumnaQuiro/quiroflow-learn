## From the Calendar

Click any empty slot on the Calendar, or use the **new appointment** button, to open the booking panel.

Fill in:

- **Appointment Type** -- determines the color, default duration, and price shown on the calendar.
- **Date** and **Time**
- **Room** and **Practitioner**
- **Patient** -- search by name, phone, or email. If they're not in the system yet, you can add them right here with first name, last name, email, and phone.
- **Note (Optional)** -- anything staff should know before the visit.
- **Repeat** -- for a recurring series (e.g. weekly).

Select **Book** (or the equivalent submit button) to save it. The appointment appears on the calendar immediately, colored by its appointment type.

## Understanding the calendar block

Each appointment block shows a small status dot -- see [Appointment statuses explained](/articles/calendar-appointments/appointment-statuses-explained) for what each color means.

## Editing or cancelling

Click an existing appointment to open it again. From there you can change the time, room, or practitioner, mark it as checked in, completed, or a no-show, or cancel it entirely.
