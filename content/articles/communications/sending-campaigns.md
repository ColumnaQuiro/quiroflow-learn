**Campaigns** lets you set up messages (WhatsApp or email) that fire automatically around something happening in the clinic, or send a one-off message to a group of patients right now.

## Automatic campaigns

Each campaign is a rule: a **trigger** plus one or more **actions**. Available triggers include:

- Appointment booked, checked in, completed, cancelled, marked as missed, or rescheduled
- Day of appointment (a morning send for anyone booked that day)
- Invoice paid
- Patient's birthday (checked daily)
- Membership started, cancelled, or a membership payment processed

For each trigger, add an action: a WhatsApp template send, or an email with your own subject and body. A single rule can have more than one action -- for example, WhatsApp *and* email on the same trigger.

Toggle a campaign on or off at any time without deleting it.

## Sending one now

Select **Send now** to message a group of patients immediately, outside of any trigger -- useful for a one-off announcement (a holiday closure, a new service) rather than an ongoing automated rule.

## Where these get logged

Every campaign send is recorded the same way as any other WhatsApp message, so it shows up in that patient's **Communications** tab and in the shared **Inbox**.
