As well as WhatsApp, the **Inbox** can receive direct messages to your Instagram professional account, Messenger messages to your Facebook Page, and messages from a chat button you put on your website. They all land in the same list, with a channel badge (**IG**, **FB**, **WEB**), and the team answers from the same place. If the AI receptionist is on, it can answer on these channels too.

It is set up in **Settings › Communication › WhatsApp**, below the templates. You need the **Open Settings** and **Communication and Growth** permissions.

## Connecting Instagram and Messenger

1. Under **Instagram messages**, click **Connect Instagram**.
2. Sign in with Meta and tick your Instagram account and the Facebook Page it is linked to.
3. If Meta shares more than one account, QuiroFlow asks "One more step: which Instagram account is this clinic's?". Pick yours; nothing is connected until you do.
4. When it's done, the status changes to **Receiving and replying**.

**Facebook Messenger** comes with that same connection: the Facebook Page you ticked receives Messenger in the Inbox too. There is no separate button.

> **Good to know:** Instagram and Messenger have no templates. You can only reply within 24 hours of the person's last message; after that the Inbox blocks the reply until they write again.

## Adding the chat to your website

The chat is a button on your website. Whoever writes through it appears in the Inbox as a new contact, and the conversation stays there.

::help-video{clip="comms--web-chat" caption="Switching on the website chat and copying its code"}
::

1. Make sure your clinic has a booking page address (in **Settings › Online Booking**). Without one the chat can't be switched on.
2. Under **Chat on your website**, turn on **Show the chat on my website**.
3. Change the **Greeting** if you like: it's the first thing a visitor reads when they open the chat.
4. Under **Code for your website**, click **Copy**.
5. Click **Save changes**.
6. Paste the code once, before `</body>`, on every page of your website. Whoever manages your website can do it in a minute.

If you later turn off **Show the chat on my website**, the button disappears from your site even where the code is still pasted.

> **Good to know:** the chat recognises a visitor by their browser. Coming back from the same browser continues the same conversation; from another device it starts a new one.

## How they appear in the Inbox

- With **Growth**, everyone who writes on these channels is a lead in **Growth › Leads**, and their conversation shows in the Inbox marked **Lead**. From there you can **Convert to patient** once they book. See [Leads](/articles/growth/leads).
- Without Growth, the conversation shows as one with someone who isn't a patient yet, and you can link it to a record from the right-hand panel.

For everything else (replying, internal notes, assigning, snoozing) see [The Inbox](/articles/communications/the-shared-inbox).
