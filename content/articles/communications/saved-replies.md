**Saved replies** are answers the team inserts in the Inbox instead of retyping them: opening hours, prices, how to find you, what the first visit includes. They are shared: one person creates them and the whole team sees and uses them.

They're written in **Settings › Communication › Saved Replies**. You need the **Open Settings** and **Communication and Growth** permissions; to use them, the **Inbox** permission is enough.

## Creating a reply

::help-video{clip="comms--saved-reply-create" caption="Creating a saved reply"}
::

1. Go to **Settings › Communication › Saved Replies**.
2. Click **New reply**.
3. Type a short **Name** for it (for example "Horario").
4. Type the **Message**. Below it you see how it will look **In the Inbox**.
5. Click **Save**.

To change one, open it in the list, edit it and click **Save**. To remove it, click **Delete** and confirm with **Delete reply**: it disappears from the Inbox for the whole team, and messages already sent with it are not affected.

## Using it in the Inbox

1. Open the conversation.
2. Click the **Saved replies** icon next to the reply box.
3. Pick the reply: its text goes into the box and you can adjust it (to add the patient's name, say).
4. Click **Send**.

There's a clip of this in [The Inbox](/articles/communications/the-shared-inbox).

> **Good to know:** a saved reply is free text, so on WhatsApp it can only be sent within 24 hours of the patient's last message. Outside that window you need a template.

> **Good to know:** saved replies don't fill in details on their own (name, date). For a personalised automatic message, use [automations](/articles/communications/automations).
