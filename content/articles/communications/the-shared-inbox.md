The **Inbox** shows every WhatsApp conversation your clinic has had with patients, whether it started from an automatic message (a confirmation, reminder, or recall) or a patient messaging you directly.

![Inbox showing a list of WhatsApp conversations with recent message previews](/screenshots/inbox.png)

## Why it's shared

Any staff member with inbox access can see and reply to any conversation -- so a patient messaging about rescheduling doesn't get missed just because a specific staff member is out. New messages are highlighted so nothing sits unread for long, and the sidebar shows an unread count so it's obvious at a glance whether anything needs attention.

This shared visibility is a deliberate trade-off against a more private, per-staff inbox: it favors nothing falling through the cracks over any one person "owning" a conversation. If your clinic wants some conversations kept private to specific staff, that's a role-permissions decision rather than an Inbox setting -- see [Roles & permissions](/articles/team-roles/roles-and-permissions).

## Starting a new conversation

Select **+ New** to start a conversation with a patient who hasn't messaged you first -- search for them the same way you would when booking, then send a free-form message if you're within their 24-hour window, or a template if not (see below).

## Replying

Open a conversation and reply like a normal chat. Replies sent within 24 hours of the patient's last message can be free-form text; outside that window, WhatsApp's rules require sending an approved template instead (the same ones configured in [Connecting WhatsApp](/articles/communications/connecting-whatsapp)). QuiroFlow shows you which mode you're in so you're not caught out by a message silently failing to send.

## Automatic status updates

When a patient replies to a confirmation or reminder message, QuiroFlow reads it automatically and updates the relevant appointment -- see [Appointment statuses explained](/articles/calendar-appointments/appointment-statuses-explained). You don't need to do anything for that to happen, but the conversation is still visible here for context -- useful when a patient's reply doesn't cleanly match an expected pattern and a human needs to read what they actually said.

## Filtering and finding conversations

Use the search box to find a conversation by patient name, phone number, or message text rather than scrolling the full list. Above the list, filter chips narrow it down further:

- **Unread** -- conversations with a message you haven't opened yet.
- **Awaiting us** -- the patient replied and it's your team's turn to respond.
- **Awaiting patient** -- you've replied and are waiting to hear back.
- **Label** -- filter by a label you've applied to conversations (e.g. to tag conversations needing follow-up, or by reason for contact). Labels are created and applied per conversation; use whatever set makes sense for how your front desk triages messages.

**Awaiting us** is worth checking first thing each day -- it's the fastest way to see exactly which patients are waiting on a reply, without reading through conversations that don't need action.

## Archiving conversations

The archive icon next to the search box switches between your regular inbox and **Archived** conversations. Archiving is per-user -- clearing a conversation out of your own view doesn't archive it for the rest of your team, so it's a personal tidying tool rather than a way to mark something as globally resolved. A patient replying to an archived conversation brings it back to the regular inbox automatically.

## What doesn't show up here

Campaign sends and appointment confirmations/reminders all appear in the Inbox as part of the relevant patient's conversation -- there's no separate "automated messages" feed to check elsewhere. If you're looking for a message you know was sent but can't find it, search by the patient rather than by message content.
