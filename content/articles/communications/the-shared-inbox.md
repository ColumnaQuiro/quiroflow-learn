The **Inbox** shows every WhatsApp conversation your clinic has had with patients, whether it started from an automatic message (a confirmation, reminder, or recall) or a patient messaging you directly.

## Why it's shared

Any staff member with inbox access can see and reply to any conversation -- so a patient messaging about rescheduling doesn't get missed just because a specific staff member is out. New messages are highlighted so nothing sits unread for long.

![Inbox showing a list of WhatsApp conversations with recent message previews](/screenshots/inbox.png)

## Replying

Open a conversation and reply like a normal chat. Replies sent within 24 hours of the patient's last message can be free-form text; outside that window, WhatsApp's rules require sending an approved template instead (the same ones configured in [Connecting WhatsApp](/articles/communications/connecting-whatsapp)).

## Automatic status updates

When a patient replies to a confirmation or reminder message, QuiroFlow reads it automatically and updates the relevant appointment -- see [Appointment statuses explained](/articles/calendar-appointments/appointment-statuses-explained). You don't need to do anything for that to happen, but the conversation is still visible here for context.
