The **Inbox** brings together every conversation the clinic has with patients and with people who aren't patients yet: **WhatsApp**, **Instagram**, **Messenger**, **Website chat** and messages from the patient **App**. The whole team works from the same list: you reply from here, leave internal notes, share conversations out and set aside the ones that can wait. Email does not go through the Inbox.

You'll find it in the menu under **Patients › Inbox**, with a count of unread messages. You need the **Inbox** permission. The staff mobile app has its own Inbox that works the same way.

> **Good to know:** the first time, QuiroFlow offers **Enable notifications** so you're told when a patient messages you, even with the tab in the background.

## Replying to a patient

::help-video{clip="inbox--reply-saved-reply" caption="Replying with a saved reply"}
::

1. Open the conversation in the list on the left.
2. Type in **Type a message…**, or click the **Saved replies** icon to insert a ready-made answer and adjust it.
3. Click **Send**.

You can also **Attach a file** (up to 16 MB, WhatsApp only) or **Record a voice note**. The **Actions** menu in the header has **Book a visit**, **Put the booking link in the reply**, **Put a payment link in the reply** (you pick **Which invoice?**) and **Open their record**. Links go in the reply box: nothing is sent until you press **Send**.

Saved replies are written in **Settings › Communication › Saved Replies**; see [Saved replies](/articles/communications/saved-replies).

## After 24 hours: sending a template

WhatsApp only allows free-text replies within 24 hours of the patient's last message. After that you'll see "More than 24h since … last messaged — free-form replies are blocked by WhatsApp. Send a template instead."

::help-video{clip="inbox--send-template" caption="Sending a template once more than 24 hours have passed"}
::

1. Click **Send template** in the banner.
2. Pick the template and fill in its details if it asks for any.
3. Click **Send**.

To message someone who has never written to you, click **New**, search for the patient and start the same way, with a template. The **Expiring** filter shows conversations whose 24 hours are about to close, so you can answer in time.

> **Good to know:** Instagram and Messenger have no templates. Outside the 24 hours, replying is blocked until the person writes again.

## Internal notes, assigning and snoozing

::help-video{clip="inbox--note-assign-snooze" caption="Leaving a note, assigning the conversation and snoozing it"}
::

- **Internal note**: click **Internal note** above the reply box, type and click **Save note**. Only the team sees it, never the patient. Type @ and a colleague's name to mention them; they get a notification.
- **Assign**: in the header, open **Assign to** and pick the person (or **Unassigned**). The whole team sees who it's assigned to, and if the patient writes again it stays with the same person.
- **Snooze (just for you)**: choose **Later today**, **Tomorrow**, **Next week** or **Another time**. The conversation leaves your list and comes back then, or sooner if they write.
- **Labels**: click **Labels** to tag it (for example "Mutua"), or create one with **New label…**.

> **Good to know:** archiving, snoozing, labels and read/unread are **yours alone**: your colleagues still see the conversation as it was. Assigning, on the other hand, is seen by the whole team. If the patient writes again, an archived or snoozed conversation comes back on its own.

With **Select several** you can assign, archive or mark many conversations unread at once.

## Finding what matters: filters

Click **Filters** to choose what you see:

- **Whose**: **All**, **Mine** or **Unassigned**.
- **Needs reply** (on by default): only where the patient wrote last, or that you marked unread. Turn it off to see everything.
- **Unread**, **Expiring** and, with the AI receptionist, **Draft ready** and **Needs human**.
- With Growth, **Patients** or **Leads**.
- Under **More filters**: **Awaiting patient**, a label, and what the AI is handling.

**Show my archived conversations** and **Show what I snoozed** bring back what you set aside. The search box (**Search name, number, messages…**) also searches inside messages.

## A number that isn't a patient's

If a number writes in that matches no record, the right-hand panel shows **Number without a patient**. You can:

- **Link to a patient**: search by **Name or surname** and click **Link**. The conversation moves to their record.
- **Create patient with this number**: type **First name** and **Surname** and click **Create and link**.

If the clinic has Growth, someone who writes in without being a patient also appears as a lead in Growth; see [Leads](/articles/growth/leads).

## The AI receptionist in the Inbox

With the AI receptionist you'll see **AI handling** on some conversations, drafts waiting for your approval (**Approve and send**) and a **Take over** button to answer yourself. See [The AI receptionist](/articles/growth/ai-receptionist).

## What the Inbox does on its own

- Replies to reminders (confirming, asking to change) are read automatically and update the appointment; the conversation stays visible.
- If someone replies "No, gracias", BAJA or STOP, they're marked **Do not contact** and stop receiving automatic messages. See [Marketing consent and unsubscribing](/articles/communications/marketing-consent-and-unsubscribe).
- Messages from an automation in test mode are marked "Not sent ·": they were recorded, but didn't go out.
