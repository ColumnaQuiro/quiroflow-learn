Go to **Settings &rarr; Clinics** to see every location on your account -- most practices start with one, created during [account setup](/articles/getting-started/creating-your-clinic), and can add more here.

## Editing a clinic

Name, address, and default appointment slot duration can all be edited directly in the clinics table -- select a field to change it in place.

![Clinics settings page listing one clinic, with an Add Clinic form below](/screenshots/settings-clinics.png)

## Adding a clinic

Use the form to create a new one, giving it a name to start.

## Business hours

Hours and availability for online booking aren't set here -- they live in **Settings &rarr; Online Booking &rarr; Clinics & Hours**, alongside the rest of your online booking configuration. See [Setting up online booking](/articles/online-booking/setting-up-online-booking).
