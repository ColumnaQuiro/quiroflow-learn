**Settings** (at the bottom of the side menu) is where the whole clinic is configured: locations, team, appointment types, payments, messages, imports... What you change here affects the whole team. What is only yours (your photo, your language, your password) is in **Account Settings**, in the menu under your photo: see [Your account](/articles/getting-started/account-settings).

## Who sees what

To enter Settings you need the **Open Settings** permission, and each group of pages also asks for its own permission (they're in the tables). Owners see everything. Pages your role can't open don't appear: neither in the menu nor on the Settings home page. See [Roles and permissions](/articles/team-roles/roles-and-permissions).

On the left you have the Settings menu, grouped; on a phone it opens with the **Sections** button. The Settings home page shows a card for each page, but not all of them: **Gift vouchers**, **Third parties**, **Education** and **Privacy** are only in the menu on the left.

## Billing

**Billing** permission, except VeriFactu (owners only).

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Services & Products** | What you charge for besides appointments |
| **Products & stock** | What you sell over the counter |
| **Packages / Bonos** | The session bonos you sell |
| **Gift vouchers** | Gift cards |
| **Memberships** | Recurring payment plans |
| **Third parties** | Insurers and others who pay for the patient |
| **Payments** | Payment methods and card payments (Stripe) |
| **Invoicing** | Tax details, numbering, IVA and what receipts show |
| **VeriFactu** | Sending the invoicing records to the AEAT |

All of this is explained in [Billing and payments](/articles/billing-payments) and [Facturas and VeriFactu](/articles/invoices-verifactu).

## Communication

**Communication and Growth** permission.

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Messages** | Appointment confirmations and reminders |
| **WhatsApp** | The WhatsApp connection and its templates |
| **Saved Replies** | Texts the team inserts in the Inbox |
| **Docs** | Consent and form templates |
| **Letters** | Letter templates on your letterhead |
| **Leads** | How new contacts move along and who gets notified |
| **Recalls** | Who follows up patients who don't come back, and the reasons |

Watch the word: here **Recalls** are the patients it's time to call back (what other programs call *recalls*), not the appointment reminders, which are under **Messages**. See [Communications](/articles/communications) and [Recalls](/articles/calendar-appointments/recalls).
