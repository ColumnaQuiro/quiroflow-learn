A closure is a whole day (or several) when the location doesn't open, for the whole team: it shows in the calendar and those days aren't offered on online booking. QuiroFlow can add the official holidays for you; local ones and the clinic's own holidays you add yourself. It's all on each location's page, in **Settings → Clinics**, under **Closures**.

You need the **Clinic and diary** permission.

## Closing on official holidays

::help-video{clip="settings--holidays" caption="Closing on the region's official holidays"}
::

1. Go to **Settings → Clinics** and open the location.
2. Under **Holidays and closures**, choose your **Comunidad autónoma** (region).
3. Turn on **Close on the official holidays**.

QuiroFlow adds the national holidays and those of your region, as published in the BOE (Spain's official gazette), for this year and the following ones. Each appears in the list as a closure tagged **Official holiday**. If you open on one some year, remove it from the list and that day stays open.

> **Good to know:** your town's two local holidays aren't in the BOE (on the islands, nor is your island's own holiday): add them by hand, as below. Next year's holidays are published in October and added by themselves.

## Adding a local holiday or a closure

::help-video{clip="settings--closure" caption="Adding a local holiday as a closure"}
::

1. In the same section, click **Add a holiday or closure…**.
2. Choose **From** and **To (included)**. For a single day, use the same date in both.
3. Add a **Reason (optional)** if you like: "Local holiday", "August holidays"…
4. Click **Add closure**. It saves at once.

## Reopening a day

Click **Remove** on the closure and confirm with **Remove closure**. Its free slots can be booked again straight away, online included.

> **Good to know:** closing a day hides the free slots but doesn't move anyone. If there were already appointments on those days, QuiroFlow tells you how many; you'll need to move or cancel them yourself from the calendar.

> **Good to know:** a closure is for the whole location. If just one person is away, record an absence on their page ([Team absences](/articles/team-roles/absences)) or block their calendar ([Recurring appointments and blocking time](/articles/calendar-appointments/recurring-and-blocking-time)).
