If your clinic has several rooms or treatment tables, create them in **Settings → Calendar Resources**. Appointments are booked into a room and the calendar can show them by room, so you can see at a glance what's taken. Rooms are grouped by clinic; a clinic with no rooms books without one.

You need the **Clinic and diary** permission.

## Adding a room

::help-video{clip="settings--rooms" caption="Adding a room to the clinic"}
::

1. Go to **Settings → Calendar Resources**.
2. Under the clinic, click **Add room**.
3. Type the name (for example, "Room 2") and click **Add room**.

Each room shows how many appointments it has this week.

## Renaming or deleting a room

- The pencil changes its name; click **Save**.
- The bin deletes it, after you confirm with **Delete room**.

> **Good to know:** deleting a room keeps its past appointments, which are simply left without a room. Time blocked on that room is deleted with it.

> **Good to know:** every new clinic starts with one room already created. Rename it to whatever you call it.

To book into a room and see the calendar by room, see [Calendar & appointments](/articles/calendar-appointments).
