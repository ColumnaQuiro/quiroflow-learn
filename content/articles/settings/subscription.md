**Subscription** (in your account menu, top right) shows your clinic's QuiroFlow plan, what it includes, the add-ons, the next payment and the invoices QuiroFlow has issued you. Anyone on the team can open the page, but only the account's **owner** sees billing and can change the plan; everyone else sees who that is so they can ask them.

## The plans

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (per month) | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | 45 € | 40 € | One practitioner, one clinic |
| **Practice** | 99 € | 89 € | One clinic with 4 practitioner seats; includes Growth |
| **Clinic** | 149 € | 134 € | Unlimited clinics and practitioner seats; includes Growth |

Prices exclude IVA. Paying for the whole year saves 10 %. Each extra practitioner seat is 19 € a month + IVA. Front desk, practice managers and bookkeepers **never use a seat**: invite as many as you like. A second clinic needs the **Clinic** plan.

## Add-ons

- **Growth**: lead capture from your ads, the AI receptionist, automations and reputation. Included in **Practice** and **Clinic**; on **Solo** it's added separately (39 € a month + IVA, or 35 € a month paying annually).
- **AI receptionist**: answers your WhatsApp, replies to patients and new enquiries, offers free times and books them. 19 € a month + IVA with 1,000 replies a month; each further 1,000 (or part of one) is 15 € + IVA, charged at the start of the next month. Included in Growth.
- **VeriFactu**: from the day you go live, 7,50 € + IVA per location per month ([Setting up VeriFactu](/articles/invoices-verifactu/setting-up-verifactu)).

To add or remove an add-on, on the **Add-ons** card click **Add** or **Remove**. Before you confirm we tell you what will be charged: "Charged today, prorated, IVA included: …" or that nothing is charged today and the change appears on your next invoice. Click **Confirm and add** or **Confirm and remove**.

## The free trial

New clinics start with a 15-day free trial, with everything included and no seat limit. A banner reminds you how many days are left ("N days left in your free trial"). To carry on afterwards, click **Add payment method**: the first charge is made when the trial ends. During the trial, add-ons show **In your trial** and a **Keep after the trial** button.

## Changing plan or seats

1. On **Subscription**, click **Change plan**.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Annual** and the plan.
3. Under **Practitioner seats**, add or remove extra seats.
4. Check the add-ons and the total.
5. Click **Confirm change**.

The charge goes to the card you have in Stripe; QuiroFlow never stores it. To change the card, click **Update card**.

## Your details and your invoices

The page has three views:

- **Plan**: what you have, what you use (for example, seats taken) and the **Next payment**.
- **Billing**: the **Billing details** QuiroFlow invoices you with (billed to, **NIF**, address and the **Billing email** invoices go to). They're changed with **Manage in Stripe**. This is also where **Cancel subscription** is: it stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.
- **Payments**: every charge, with its **Invoice PDF**. You can filter by year and **Download all invoices**.

> **Good to know:** if a payment fails, you'll see "Your last payment failed" with the date of the next attempt. Your calendar and records stay accessible; online booking and automatic reminders pause until the payment goes through. Update your card to sort it out.

> **Good to know:** these are QuiroFlow's invoices to your clinic. Your clinic's facturas to its patients are covered in [Receipts, facturas and rectifying facturas](/articles/invoices-verifactu/receipts-and-invoices).
