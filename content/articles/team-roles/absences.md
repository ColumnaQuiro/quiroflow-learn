When someone on the team is going to be away (sick leave, parental leave, holidays, a personal day, a course), record it as an absence on their page. They stay on the team and keep everything that's theirs, but while they're away their hours aren't offered: not on online booking, not on the waitlist, and not by the AI receptionist, which offers their patients a colleague instead. For someone who doesn't see patients, the absence shows on the **Turnos** tab of the working time log.

Only an owner can mark someone as away.

## Marking someone as away

::help-video{clip="team--absence" caption="Recording sick leave with no return date"}
::

1. Go to **Settings → Team** and open the person's page.
2. Under **Away**, click **Mark … as away** (with their name).
3. Choose the **Type**: **Sick leave**, **Parental leave**, **Personal day**, **Holiday** or **Training**.
4. Under **From**, the first day they're off.
5. Under **Back on (optional)**, the day they're back at work. Leave it empty and the absence lasts until you mark them back; with a date, it ends by itself that morning.
6. Add a **Note for the team (optional)** if you like.
7. Click **Save**.

Their page and the team list show the person with the type of absence.

> **Good to know:** a **Holiday** always needs the day they're back.

## Their appointments during the absence

Recording an absence doesn't move or cancel the appointments they already had. If they have visits in those days, their page tells you how many are still booked. Click **Move or cancel them**: you can pass them to a colleague (each one only if that colleague is free at that time) or cancel them, in which case your clinic's cancellation automations tell the patients.

If someone is off unexpectedly and today's appointments are what matters, use **Unplanned absence** from the calendar. See [Recurring appointments and blocking time](/articles/calendar-appointments/recurring-and-blocking-time).

## When they're back

On the current absence you have:

- **Back today**: ends it right now.
- **Set return date** or **Change return date**: if you now know when they're back.
- **Remove**: deletes a future absence that's no longer happening.

> **Good to know:** an absence isn't the same as deactivating someone. Deactivating is for someone who leaves the clinic: they can't sign in and their seat is freed. See [Managing team members](/articles/team-roles/managing-team-members).
