In Spain every employer has to keep a daily record of its team's working hours (article 34.9 of the Workers' Statute). In QuiroFlow each person clocks themselves from **My Day** or from **Working time** in the sidebar (**Today** › **Working time**), and the owner reviews the whole team's month, approves corrections and holidays, and exports it. Records are kept for four years and never deleted.

## Clock in and out

::help-video{clip="jornada--clock-in" caption="Clocking in, taking a break and coming back"}
::

1. When you start, click **Clock in** on the clock card (it's at the top of **My Day** and of **Working time**).
2. For a break, click **Break**; when you're back, **Back from break**.
3. When you finish, click **Clock out**. If you come back later (in the afternoon, say), click **Clock in again**.

The card shows your day: "In 09:01 · Break 11:00–11:15", and whether you're working or on a break.

> **Good to know:** the time is set by the server, not typed in. If you forgot to clock, ask for a correction (below). If an earlier day was left without a clock-out, the card reminds you: "An earlier day was left without a clock-out. Ask for a correction in My month."

## Ask for a correction

::help-video{clip="jornada--correction" caption="Asking for a correction for a clock-out that was missed"}
::

1. Open **My month** (the link on the clock card, or the **Mine** tab in **Working time**).
2. Find the day and click **Ask for a correction**.
3. Under **What to fix** choose the wrong entry, or **Add a missing** in, break, back or out.
4. Give the **Real time** and the **Reason** (for example "I forgot to clock out").
5. Click **Send to the owner**.

"Sent to the owner. Your original entry stays as it was until they approve it." Meanwhile the day shows **Correction pending**, and under **Your corrections** you can see whether it's **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected**. For a day with nothing clocked at all, use **A day with nothing clocked?**.

## Ask for holiday or leave

Under **Mine** you also see your **Holiday** (days left this year; 22 if the owner hasn't set another number), **My shifts this week** and **My requests**.

1. Click **Ask for holiday or leave**.
2. Choose the **Type**: **Holiday**, **Personal day**, **Sick leave**, **Parental leave** or **Training**.
3. Set **From** and **To** (or tick **Half a day only** for a single day). You'll see how many working days it is and how many you'd have left.
4. Add a **Note (optional)** and click **Send to the owner**.

While it's pending you can **Withdraw** the request.

## For owners: the team's month

Owners see three tabs: **Month**, **Shifts** and **Mine**.

::help-video{clip="jornada--approve-correction" caption="Approving a correction and looking at one person's month in detail"}
::

On **Month**:

1. At the top, under **Corrections waiting**, click **Approve** or **Reject** on each one.
2. The table shows each person with their hours for **Today**, **This week** and **This month**, the **Days** and their **Status** (**Working**, **On a break** or **Clocked out**).
3. Click a person to see their month in detail, day by day: **In**, **Breaks**, **Out**, **Total** and **Notes** (for example **No clock-out** or **Corrected**, with the earlier time and who asked).
4. Export the month with **Export CSV** or **PDF to sign** (one page per person, to sign).

If someone doesn't have to clock (a self-employed practitioner renting a room, say), tick **Doesn't have to clock (self-employed)** in their detail: their clock card disappears and they leave the export.

> **Good to know:** an approved correction doesn't erase the original entry: it stays alongside, with who asked for it and who approved it.

## For owners: shifts and holidays

::help-video{clip="jornada--approve-leave" caption="Approving a holiday and blocking the calendar"}
::

On **Shifts**:

- A table with each person's shift for the week. Click a day to change that day only (write it like 09:00-14:00, 16:00-19:00; empty means off) and **Save**, or go back to their usual hours with **Usual**. The weekly pattern is changed in **Settings › Team**.
- **Requests waiting**: each request shows how many working days it is, how many they have left and how many visits they have booked on those days. Click **Approve and block the calendar** or **Reject**.
- If they have visits on those days when you approve, you'll see that their calendar is already blocked but the visits still need moving. **Move or cancel them** opens [Unplanned absence](/articles/calendar-appointments/recurring-and-blocking-time) already filled in.
- **Holiday this year**: the days each person has (you can change them), taken, approved and left.
- **Clocked against the shift**: days when what was clocked is more than 15 minutes off the shift. It only informs: what was clocked stays as it is.

> **Good to know:** approving a holiday blocks the calendar but doesn't move visits: do that with **Move or cancel them**.

> **Good to know:** only working days count, Monday to Friday. Days the whole clinic is closed don't use up holiday.

> **Good to know:** a shift is different from the hours a practitioner can be booked. The shift is set in **Settings › Team**, on the person's page (**Their shift**); booking hours under **Their hours**. See [Practitioner working hours](/articles/team-roles/practitioner-working-hours).
