Everyone at your clinic signs in to QuiroFlow with their own login. To add someone you send them an invite from **Settings → Team**: they get a link and, on opening it, create their login and land straight in your clinic with the role you chose. Don't share one login between several people: that way each person sees what's theirs and the [activity log](/articles/security-privacy/activity-log) knows who did what.

You need the **Team** permission (owners always have it).

## Inviting someone

::help-video{clip="team--invite" caption="Inviting a receptionist who doesn't see patients"}
::

1. Go to **Settings → Team** and click **Invite**. **Invite to the team** opens.
2. **Email**: we send the link to that address. If they have no email, leave it empty and share the link yourself (over WhatsApp, for example).
3. **Name (optional)**: so you recognise the invite in the list.
4. **Role**: **Owner**, **Practitioner**, **Front Desk** or any role you've created. It decides what they can see and do; you can change it any time. See [Roles and permissions](/articles/team-roles/roles-and-permissions).
5. **Will see patients**: leave it ticked if they'll be treating. They show in the calendar and take a practitioner seat once they accept. Untick it for front desk and admin staff, who take no seat.
6. **Clinics**: if you have more than one, tick where they'll work (at least one).
7. Click **Create invite**.

**Invite created** tells you whether the email went out and shows the link, with **Copy** in case you want to send it yourself too. If the email couldn't be sent, the invite does exist: click **Retry email** or share the link.

> **Good to know:** seats are checked when the person **accepts**, not when the invite is created. If your plan has no free seats, the dialog warns you: untick **Will see patients** or add a seat. During the free trial there's no limit. See [Your subscription](/articles/settings/subscription).

## Accepting an invite

This is what the invited person does:

1. Open the link from the email (its subject reads "You're invited to join…" followed by your clinic's name) or the one you sent them.
2. They see **You've been invited to QuiroFlow**. If they already have a login, they click **Sign in**; if not, **Create an account** and sign up with their email and a password.
3. They land on your clinic's **Dashboard** with the invite's role.

> **Good to know:** if the invite is for someone who will see patients and there are no seats left, opening the link tells them your plan has no free practitioner seat. Add a seat (or change the invite so they don't see patients) and ask them to open the same link again.

## Pending invites

In **Settings → Team**, **Pending invites** lists the ones nobody has accepted yet. For each you can:

- **Copy link**, to send it yourself.
- **Resend email**, if the invite had an address.
- **Revoke**: the link stops working. You can invite that person again whenever you like.

> **Good to know:** invites don't expire, and whoever opens the link joins with that role. Revoke the ones you no longer want.

## Linking imported names

If you imported your data from another system, some appointments may arrive under the name of a practitioner who doesn't have a QuiroFlow login yet. They show in **Imported names not linked**, with how many appointments each name has.

::help-video{clip="team--link-imported" caption="Linking imported appointments to a team member"}
::

- If that person is already on the team: pick them in **Link to…**, click **Link** and confirm. Their imported appointments become theirs: they show in their calendar, their reports and their **My Day**. The imported name is kept on each appointment.
- If they aren't yet: click **Invite as practitioner**. The invite opens ready-made, and the appointments become theirs as soon as they accept.
